# Taming Silicon Valley: How We Can Ensure That AI Works for Us

## Citation

Marcus, Gary F. Taming Silicon Valley: How We Can Ensure That AI Works for Us. Cambridge, MA: The MIT Press, 2024. https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262551069/taming-silicon-valley/.

## One-line summary

A short policy manifesto arguing that Big Tech profits while society absorbs AI's harms, and setting out eight elements of a coherent AI policy.

## Key claims

- Big Tech takes advantage of citizens, and AI deepens the imbalance.
- Companies push the negative externalities of AI onto the public without paying for them.
- The pursuit of growth drives a "moral descent" away from early good intentions.
- A coherent AI policy needs data rights, layered oversight, and meaningful tax reform.
- Ordinary citizens can and must push for the safeguards the moment requires.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The book turns his technical critique into an organising programme aimed at citizens, not researchers.
- Framing harms as externalities shows him applying an economic lens to AI governance.
- The eight-point policy list mirrors the layered, FDA-style oversight he urged in Senate testimony.
