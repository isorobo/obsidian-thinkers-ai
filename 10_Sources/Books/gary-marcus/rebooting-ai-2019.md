# Rebooting AI: Building Artificial Intelligence We Can Trust

## Citation

Marcus, Gary, and Ernest Davis. Rebooting AI: Building Artificial Intelligence We Can Trust. New York: Pantheon Books, 2019. https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/603982/rebooting-ai-by-gary-marcus-and-ernest-davis/.

## One-line summary

Marcus and Davis argue that current AI is too narrow to be trusted and that common sense and deep understanding, not bigger data sets, are the way forward.

## Key claims

- AI achievements so far sit in closed systems with fixed rules, too narrow for genuine intelligence.
- Trustworthy AI requires common sense and deep understanding rather than statistical analysis alone.
- Gathering ever larger data sets is not the route to reliable intelligence.
- Inspiration from the human mind shows what is needed to advance AI to the next level.
- Wise design removes the need to fear a future of machine overlords.

## Excerpts

> "The achievements in the field thus far have occurred in closed systems with fixed sets of rules, and these approaches are too narrow to achieve genuine intelligence."

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The book sets out the trust-and-common-sense thesis that anchors his later policy and capability work.
- Drawing on the human mind continues the cognitive-science grounding seen across his papers.
- Rejecting bigger data as the answer prefigures the scaling-limits argument he presses through the 2020s.
