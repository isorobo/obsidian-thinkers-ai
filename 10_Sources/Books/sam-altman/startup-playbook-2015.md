# Startup Playbook

## Citation

Altman, Sam. *Startup Playbook*. Y Combinator, 2015. https://playbook.samaltman.com/.

## One-line summary

Written while running Y Combinator, the playbook distils Altman's advice on building a product users love, raising money, and managing cash.

## Key claims

- A startup's goal is to make something users love before pursuing growth.
- Mediocre teams do not build great companies.
- A great product is the one thing every great company has in common.
- Growth and momentum drive great execution.
- Focus and intensity, distilled to two words, summarise his operating advice.
- Most startups fail through internal mismanagement rather than competitor action.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Establishes the product-first, growth-obsessed operating style Altman later applies to ChatGPT's rapid scale-up.
- Frames competitive threats as secondary to internal execution failure, a view he repeats about OpenAI's rivals.
- Provides the earliest documented version of the concentrated-bet philosophy restated in What I Wish Someone Had Told Me.
