# GPT-4 Technical Report

## Citation

OpenAI (Achiam, J., Adler, S., Agarwal, S., ... Altman, S., et al.). "GPT-4 Technical Report". arXiv:2303.08774, 15 March 2023 (revised 4 March 2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08774.

## One-line summary

The technical report introduces GPT-4, a multimodal model that reaches human-level performance on several professional and academic benchmarks, and describes infrastructure that predicts model behaviour at scale.

## Key claims

- GPT-4 accepts image and text inputs and produces text outputs.
- The model reaches human-level performance on various professional and academic benchmarks.
- GPT-4 passes a simulated bar exam in roughly the top ten percent of test-takers.
- The infrastructure and optimisation methods behave predictably across a wide range of scales.
- Performance at full scale can be predicted using models trained with far less compute.
- Sam Altman is listed among approximately 280 named co-authors credited on the report.
