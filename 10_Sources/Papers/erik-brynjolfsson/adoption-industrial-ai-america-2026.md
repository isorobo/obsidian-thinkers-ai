# The Adoption of Industrial AI in America (2026)

## Citation

McElheran, K., Yang, M.-J., Kroff, Z. and Brynjolfsson, E. "The Adoption of Industrial AI in America". AEA Papers and Proceedings, vol. 116, May 2026. https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Fpandp.20261033.

## One-line summary

A Census survey of about 28,500 manufacturing establishments finds low industrial AI adoption, tied to modern digital infrastructure and organisational readiness.

## Key claims

- Only 22.8 percent of plants report any AI use as of 2021.
- Adoption tracks cloud computing and predictive analytics, not legacy IT.
- Cost and lack of a use case are the most cited barriers.
- Organisational readiness, not prior productivity, is the key barrier.

## Excerpts

> "Using a mandatory, purpose-designed Census Bureau survey of approximately 28,500 establishments, we provide new evidence on industrial AI adoption in US manufacturing."
> ~ Abstract, AEA Papers and Proceedings, May 2026

> "Despite widespread digitization, only 22.8 percent of plants report any AI use as of 2021; intensity-weighted adoption is far lower."
> ~ Abstract

> "Adoption correlates with more-recent digital infrastructure - cloud computing and predictive analytics - rather than legacy on-premises IT or descriptive analytics."
> ~ Abstract (dashes in the original replaced with hyphens)

> "Prior productivity does not predict use, pointing to organizational readiness as a key barrier to AI diffusion."
> ~ Abstract

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Locating the barrier in organisational readiness suggests a consistent view that complements, not the technology, limit diffusion.
- Using mandatory Census data reads as a preference for representative evidence over self-selected samples.
