# The Enterprise AI Playbook: Lessons from 51 Successful Developments (2026)

## Citation

Pereira, E., Graylin, A. W. and Brynjolfsson, E. "The Enterprise AI Playbook: Lessons from 51 Successful Developments". Stanford Digital Economy Lab, April 2026. https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/enterprise-ai-playbook.

## One-line summary

A study of 51 live enterprise AI cases finds that outcomes depended on the organisation, not the model.

## Key claims

- The research documents real-world cases that delivered business value.
- Outcomes varied from weeks to years with the same technology and use cases.
- The difference lay in organisational readiness, processes, leadership and willingness to change.

## Excerpts

> "We set out to build something empirical. To document real-world use cases that have actually delivered business value."
> ~ Research Methodology

> "Across 51 enterprise cases over 5 months, we found stories of transformation measured in weeks and others measured in years. Same technology, same use cases, vastly different outcomes."
> ~ Key Finding

> "The difference was never the AI model. It was always the organization. Its readiness, its processes, its leadership, its willingness to change and fail."
> ~ Critical Success Factor

> "Our ambition with this research is simple: to offer a practical window into what is actually happening inside companies as they create value with AI."
> ~ Research Purpose

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The report favours empirical case evidence over forecasts, which reads as a deliberate answer to the opening complaint about endless predictions.
- Pinning success on organisations matches his long-running complements argument.
