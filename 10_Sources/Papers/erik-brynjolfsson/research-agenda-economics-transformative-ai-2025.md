# A Research Agenda for the Economics of Transformative AI (2025)

## Citation

Brynjolfsson, E., Korinek, A. and Agrawal, A. K. "A Research Agenda for the Economics of Transformative AI". NBER Working Paper 34256, September 2025. https://www.nber.org/papers/w34256.

## One-line summary

The authors set out nine Grand Challenges for economists studying transformative AI, from growth and distribution to safety risks and transition dynamics.

## Key claims

- Understanding how transformative AI reshapes economic models, institutions and policies is urgent.
- The agenda names nine Grand Challenges spanning growth, innovation, distribution, power, geoeconomics, information, safety, well-being and transition.
- Faster work on these areas can help realise the economic potential of transformative AI.

## Excerpts

> "As we approach Transformative Artificial Intelligence (TAI), there is an urgent need to advance our understanding of how it could reshape our economic models, institutions and policies."
> ~ Abstract, NBER Working Paper 34256

> "We propose a research agenda for the economics of TAI by identifying nine Grand Challenges: economic growth, innovation, income distribution, decision-making power, geoeconomics, information flows, safety risks, human well-being, and transition dynamics."
> ~ Abstract, NBER Working Paper 34256

> "By accelerating work in these areas, researchers can develop insights and tools to help fulfill the economic potential of TAI."
> ~ Abstract, NBER Working Paper 34256

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Treating safety risks as one challenge among nine suggests he frames AI safety as an economic question, not a separate field.
- The agenda format reads as a push to organise a research community rather than to argue a single thesis.
