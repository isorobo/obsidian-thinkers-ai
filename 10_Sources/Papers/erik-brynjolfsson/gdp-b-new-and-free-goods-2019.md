# GDP-B: Accounting for the Value of New and Free Goods in the Digital Economy

## Citation

Brynjolfsson, Erik, Avinash Collis, W. Erwin Diewert, Felix Eggers, and Kevin J. Fox. "GDP-B: Accounting for the Value of New and Free Goods in the Digital Economy". National Bureau of Economic Research Working Paper No. 25695, March 2019. https://www.nber.org/papers/w25695.

## One-line summary

The authors derive a new metric, GDP-B, that quantifies the welfare benefits of new and free digital goods that conventional national accounts omit.

## Key claims

- The welfare contributions of new and free digital goods are poorly measured in current national accounts.
- GDP-B quantifies the benefits of these goods rather than their costs.
- Valuations are estimated through incentive-compatible choice experiments.
- Including the welfare gains from Facebook would add between 0.05 and 0.11 percentage points to annual GDP-B growth in the United States.

## Excerpts

> "The welfare contributions of the digital economy, characterized by the proliferation of new and free goods, are not well-measured in our current national accounts."

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The paper turns a measurement critique into a constructive proposal, a new statistic, showing his builder's bent.
- Quantifying free-goods welfare extends his lifelong argument that official statistics undercount the digital economy.
- The reliance on choice experiments confirms his preference for direct empirical estimates over theory alone.
