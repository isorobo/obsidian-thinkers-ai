# AI and the Fed (2025)

## Citation

Kazinnik, S. and Brynjolfsson, E. "AI and the Fed". NBER Working Paper 33998, July 2025. https://www.nber.org/papers/w33998.

## One-line summary

The paper shows how central banks, using the Federal Reserve as an example, can integrate AI into strategy and daily operations.

## Key claims

- AI can modernise key central banking functions from the top down.
- A task-level view finds significant potential for workforce augmentation and efficiency gains.
- Data infrastructure and workforce transitions are the main adoption challenges.

## Excerpts

> "This paper examines how central banks can strategically integrate artificial intelligence (AI) to enhance their operations."
> ~ Abstract, NBER Working Paper 33998

> "We first consider a top-down view, showing how AI can modernize key central banking functions."
> ~ Abstract, NBER Working Paper 33998

> "We then adopt a bottom-up approach focusing on the impact of generative AI on specific tasks and occupations within the Federal Reserve and find a significant potential for workforce augmentation and efficiency gains."
> ~ Abstract, NBER Working Paper 33998

> "We also address critical challenges associated with AI adoption, such as the need to upgrade data infrastructure and manage workforce transitions."
> ~ Abstract, NBER Working Paper 33998

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The task-level, bottom-up method suggests he applies his occupation-and-task framework to institutions, not only firms.
- Pairing augmentation with transition costs reads as consistent wariness about frictionless adoption.
