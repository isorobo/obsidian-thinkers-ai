# A very preliminary analysis of DALL-E 2 (2022)

## Citation

Marcus, G., Davis, E. and Aaronson, S. "A very preliminary analysis of DALL-E 2". arXiv, submitted 25 April 2022, revised 2 May 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.13807.

## One-line summary

The authors report fourteen deliberately hard tests of DALL-E 2's common sense, reasoning and text understanding, with mixed results.

## Key claims

- The tests were designed to assess common sense, reasoning and understanding of complex texts.
- The prompts were harder than typical showcased ones.
- For 5 of 14 prompts at least one of ten images satisfied the request.
- No prompt had all ten images satisfy the request.

## Excerpts

> "The DALL-E 2 system generates original synthetic images corresponding to an input text as caption."
> ~ Abstract, opening sentence

> "We report here on the outcome of fourteen tests of this system designed to assess its common sense, reasoning and ability to understand complex texts."
> ~ Abstract

> "All of our prompts were intentionally much more challenging than the typical ones that have been showcased in recent weeks."
> ~ Abstract

> "Nevertheless, for 5 out of the 14 prompts, at least one of the ten images fully satisfied our requests."
> ~ Abstract

> "On the other hand, on no prompt did all of the ten images satisfy our requests."
> ~ Abstract, closing sentence

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a preference for adversarial, hand-built tests over showcased demos.
- Suggests he reports partial successes openly, which keeps the critique evidence-based.
