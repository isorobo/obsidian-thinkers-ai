# Fundamental Principles of Linguistic Structure are Not Represented by o3 (2025)

## Citation

Murphy, E., Leivada, E., Dentella, V., Gunther, F. and Marcus, G. "Fundamental Principles of Linguistic Structure are Not Represented by o3". arXiv, submitted 15 February 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10934.

## One-line summary

The authors test OpenAI's o3 on linguistic structure and report failures in phrase structure, comparatives and acceptability, concluding that compositionality remains a wall.

## Key claims

- A successful AGI would need rapid creation of grounded compositional abstractions.
- The model fails to generalise basic phrase structure rules.
- It fails to rate and explain acceptability dynamics.
- It seems unable to represent multiple parses.

## Excerpts

> "A core component of a successful artificial general intelligence would be the rapid creation and manipulation of grounded compositional abstractions"
> ~ Abstract, opening

> "it fails to generalize basic phrase structure rules; it fails with comparative sentences involving semantically illegal cardinality comparisons"
> ~ Abstract

> "it fails to correctly rate and explain acceptability dynamics; and it fails to distinguish between instructions to generate unacceptable semantic vs. unacceptable syntactic outputs"
> ~ Abstract

> "it is seemingly incapable of representing multiple parses to evaluate against various possible semantic interpretations"
> ~ Abstract

> "deep learning is hitting a wall with respect to compositionality (Marcus 2022), but that it is hitting a stubbornly resilient wall that cannot readily be surmounted"
> ~ Abstract, closing

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a return to compositionality as the central test for general intelligence.
- Suggests he cites his own earlier claim as a hypothesis the new tests support.
