# Searching for collective behavior in a network of real neurons (2013)

## Citation

Gasper Tkacik et al. (2013). *Searching for collective behavior in a network of real neurons*. arXiv (J Stat Mech). https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.3061

## One-line summary

Maximum-entropy probabilistic models of correlated spiking activity in 120-neuron retinal populations, drawn from Amodei's Princeton neuroscience PhD lineage.

## Key claims

- Pairwise maximum-entropy models reproduce higher-order correlations in retinal ganglion cell activity to good approximation.
- The reconstructed energy landscape exhibits multiple local minima corresponding to distinct collective firing patterns.
- Approximate the network distribution sits near a critical point in parameter space, suggesting computational implications for neural coding.
- Provides empirical foundation for treating neural populations as statistical-physics ensembles.
