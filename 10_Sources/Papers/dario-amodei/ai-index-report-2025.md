# AI Index Report 2025

## Citation

Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI. (2025). *Artificial Intelligence Index Report 2025*.

## One-line summary

Annual AI Index featuring a highlight chapter on real-world economic integration of AI, built on the Anthropic Claude-conversation task analysis.

## Key claims

- Computer and mathematical occupations dominate AI use at 37.2% of interactions.
- Arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media follow at 10.3%.
- AI adoption peaks in the upper wage quartile; drops off at both wage extremes.
- Occupations requiring bachelor's-degree preparation show fifty percent higher AI usage than their workforce share.
- 57% augmentation, 43% automation split in observed usage.
- Approximately 36% of occupations use AI for at least one quarter of their tasks; only 4% use AI for 75% or more.

## Excerpts

> "Analysis of over 4 million real-world AI interactions provides comprehensive empirical evidence of how AI is being integrated across economic sectors."

> "Only about 4% of occupations show AI usage across 75% or more of their tasks, suggesting that wholesale automation of entire job categories is not yet occurring."
