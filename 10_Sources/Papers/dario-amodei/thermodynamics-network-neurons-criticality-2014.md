# Thermodynamics for a network of neurons: Signatures of criticality (2014)

## Citation

Gasper Tkacik et al. (2014). *Thermodynamics for a network of neurons: Signatures of criticality*. arXiv (PNAS). https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.5946

## One-line summary

Treats the spiking patterns of a 120-neuron retinal population as a thermodynamic ensemble and finds signatures of criticality (diverging heat capacity) in the inferred parameter manifold.

## Key claims

- The neural distribution lies near a critical point in the inferred maximum-entropy model's parameter space.
- Heat capacity peaks consistent with second-order phase transitions appear as the population is rescaled.
- Criticality may be a generic feature of biological networks balancing representational capacity against metabolic cost.
- Bridges Bialek-school statistical physics with empirical neural data analysis.
