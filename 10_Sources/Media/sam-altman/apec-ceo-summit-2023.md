# Remarks at the APEC CEO Summit (2023)

## Citation

Altman, S. Remarks at the APEC CEO Summit, San Francisco, 16 November 2023. Reported in Nix, N. "Here's what Sam Altman said at APEC, a day before he got fired from OpenAI". The San Francisco Standard, 17 November 2023. https://sfstandard.com/2023/11/17/openai-sam-altman-fired-apec-talk/.

## One-line summary

On the eve of his November 2023 removal, Altman told APEC executives that AI would drive a shift from scarcity to abundance and argued against heavy near-term regulation.

## Key claims

- Building transformative AI has been Altman's stated life's work since childhood.
- Generative AI is described as the most transformative and beneficial technology humanity has invented.
- The 2020s are framed as the decade humanity begins moving from scarcity toward abundance.
- Altman argues against heavy regulation of AI for at least the next couple of generations.
- He predicts 2024 model capability will exceed what most observers expect.
- He acknowledges AI could amplify existing misinformation problems.

## Excerpts

> "It's definitely my life's work and what I always wanted to work on since I was a little kid"
> ~ On personal motivation

> "I think this will be the most transformative and beneficial technology humanity has yet invented"
> ~ On generative AI's potential

> "The 2020s will be the decade where humanity as a whole begins the transition from scarcity to abundance"
> ~ On societal transformation

> "We don't need heavy regulation here. Probably not even for the next couple of generations"
> ~ On regulatory concerns

> "The model capability, like what these systems can do, will have taken such a leap forward no one expected"
> ~ On 2024 predictions

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Marks an early anti-regulation stance later echoed in the 2025 Senate Commerce testimony, contrasting his pro-licensing 2023 Senate Judiciary statement six months earlier.
- Uses "scarcity to abundance" language consistent with the economic thesis in Moore's Law for Everything.
- Delivered one day before his November 2023 removal, giving the talk unusual significance as his last major public statement before the crisis.
