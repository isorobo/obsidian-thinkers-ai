# An Interview with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman About DevDay and the AI Buildout

## Citation

Thompson, B. (host), Altman, S. (guest). "An Interview with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman About DevDay and the AI Buildout". Stratechery, 8 October 2025. https://stratechery.com/2025/an-interview-with-openai-ceo-sam-altman-about-devday-and-the-ai-buildout/.

## One-line summary

Altman frames OpenAI's infrastructure spending as a company-scale bet and admits the company should temper its own public hype.

## Key claims

- OpenAI's stated goal remains building AGI and eventually superintelligence.
- Altman frames large infrastructure spending as a deliberate, company-scale bet.
- ChatGPT's early growth surprised OpenAI more than any other launch outcome.
- Maintaining user trust in ChatGPT's answers is described as commercially essential.
- Altman sees large latent demand for creative tools beyond current products.
- He cautions that OpenAI should temper public hype around its own announcements.

## Excerpts

> "We're trying to build very capable AI, AGI, superintelligence, whatever it's called these days..."
> ~ On OpenAI's core mission

> "We are going to spend a lot on infrastructure, we are going to make a bet, the company scale bet that this is the right time to do it."
> ~ On infrastructure spending

> "If we ever start doing anything that is not our best effort to get the user the best answer, trust in ChatGPT...would fall precipitously."
> ~ On user trust

> "There is so much latent creative expression demand in the world..."
> ~ On creative expression

> "We should probably hype less on Twitter, we just get excited."
> ~ On public communication

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Shows rare self-criticism about OpenAI's public communication style.
- Frames infrastructure spending as existential strategy rather than routine capital allocation.
- Continues linking user trust directly to commercial survival, a recurring justification for caution in deployment.
