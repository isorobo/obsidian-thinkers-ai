# Sam Altman on Lex Fridman #419 (2024)

## Citation

Fridman, L. (host), Altman, S. (guest). "Sam Altman: OpenAI, GPT-5, Sora, Board Saga, Elon Musk, Ilya, Power & AGI". Lex Fridman Podcast, episode 419, 18 March 2024. https://lexfridman.com/sam-altman-2/.

## One-line summary

Altman calls compute the future's most valuable commodity and gives his most candid account of the November 2023 board crisis.

## Key claims

- Altman expects compute to become the most valuable commodity in the world.
- He describes the November 2023 board crisis as the most painful professional experience of his life.
- He rates GPT-4 as inadequate relative to the reasoning ability future systems will need.
- OpenAI has signalled improved reasoning as a long-standing research direction.
- Altman frames competition from Google and other labs as beneficial for faster, cheaper innovation.

## Excerpts

> "I think compute is going to be the currency of the future. I think it'll be maybe the most precious commodity in the world."
> ~ Opening section, on compute

> "That was definitely the most painful professional experience of my life, and chaotic and shameful and upsetting."
> ~ OpenAI board saga section

> "I think it kind of sucks"
> ~ GPT-4 section, approximately 45:08

> "We have said for a while that we think better reasoning in these systems is an important direction."
> ~ Q* section

> "The pros seem obvious, which is that we get better products and more innovation faster and cheaper."
> ~ Google and Gemini section

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reveals candid emotional language about the 2023 governance crisis, unusual against his typically measured public register.
- Reasserts compute scarcity as central to strategy, consistent with his later congressional testimony on infrastructure.
- Treats competitor progress as validation rather than threat, a recurring rhetorical move.
