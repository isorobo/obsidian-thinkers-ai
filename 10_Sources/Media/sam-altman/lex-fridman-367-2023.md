# Sam Altman on GPT-4, ChatGPT, and the Future of AI (Lex Fridman #367)

## Citation

Fridman, L. (host), Altman, S. (guest). "Sam Altman: OpenAI CEO on GPT-4, ChatGPT, and the Future of AI". Lex Fridman Podcast, episode 367, 25 March 2023. https://lexfridman.com/sam-altman/.

## One-line summary

Altman describes GPT-4 as an early, imperfect system, and frames iterative public deployment as OpenAI's core alignment strategy.

## Key claims

- GPT-4 is an early, slow, and imperfect system relative to future models.
- Reinforcement learning from human feedback aligns models to behave the way humans want.
- Alignment and capability are closely linked rather than fully separate goals.
- OpenAI aims to grow alignment faster than it grows raw capability.
- Altman expects many AGI systems to coexist rather than a single winner-take-all system.
- Public deployment of imperfect technology is a deliberate transparency strategy.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Frames iterative public deployment as the alignment strategy, matching the profile's stated alignment view.
- Downplays single-winner AGI race framing, a contrast with his later 2025 congressional testimony on US-China competition.
- Treats capability and alignment as entangled, not sequential, problems.
