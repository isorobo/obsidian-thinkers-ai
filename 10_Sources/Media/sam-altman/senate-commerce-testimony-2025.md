# Senate Commerce Testimony - Sam Altman (2025)

## Citation

Altman, S. (2025). Testimony before the US Senate Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation, hearing on "Winning the AI Race: Strengthening U.S. Capabilities in Computing and Innovation", 8 May 2025.

## One-line summary

Altman tells the Senate Commerce Committee that a single light-touch federal framework, not a state-by-state patchwork, is essential to winning the AI race.

## Key claims

- ChatGPT serves more than 500 million weekly users.
- A pre-approval regulatory process for AI models would be disastrous for US competitiveness.
- Certainty on permitting for data centres and electricity supply is essential to build AI infrastructure.
- Complying with fifty different state-level regulatory regimes would be very difficult.
- Altman favours one light-touch federal framework over a state patchwork.
- Altman states he does not want his child's best friend to be an AI bot.
- US technology diffusion through consumer products is presented as a form of global influence.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Marks a shift from the 2023 pro-licensing posture toward light-touch federal preemption.
- Prioritises infrastructure and permitting over safety testing in this appearance, unlike the 2023 hearing.
- Still voices personal caution about AI's social role, even while opposing formal restrictions on companies.
