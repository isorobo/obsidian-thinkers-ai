# OpenAI DevDay 2023 Opening Keynote

## Citation

Altman, S. "OpenAI DevDay 2023 Opening Keynote". OpenAI DevDay, San Francisco, 6 November 2023. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-NytuE3iLhY.

## One-line summary

At OpenAI's first developer conference, Altman recaps a year of rapid growth and commits the company to gradual, iterative deployment.

## Key claims

- ChatGPT shipped as a low-key research preview on 30 November 2022 and grew far beyond expectations.
- GPT-4 launched in March 2023 and remained the most capable model available at the time.
- Developers report that GPT-4 costs too much for many applications they want to build.
- OpenAI's new GPTs let users program custom behaviour through natural language alone.
- OpenAI commits to gradual, iterative deployment as its core release philosophy.
- Better tools are presented as a route to letting people build extraordinary things.

## Excerpts

> "About a year ago, November 30th, we shipped ChatGPT as a low-key research preview, and that went pretty well."
> ~ On ChatGPT's launch

> "We've heard from developers that there are a lot of things that they want to build, but GPT-4 just costs too much."
> ~ On pricing feedback

> "You can in effect program a GPT with language just by talking to it."
> ~ On GPTs

> "We really believe in the importance of gradual iterative deployment."
> ~ On deployment philosophy

> "We believe that if you give people better tools, they will do amazing things."
> ~ On technology's purpose

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Frames rapid iteration and price cuts as evidence of a deliberate strategy, not just competitive pressure.
- Repeats the "gradual iterative deployment" language central to his stated alignment view.
- Positions cost reduction, echoing Moore's Law for Everything, as core product strategy rather than only economic theory.
