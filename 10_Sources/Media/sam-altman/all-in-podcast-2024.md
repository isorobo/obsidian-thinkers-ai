# In Conversation with Sam Altman (All-In Podcast, 2024)

## Citation

Palihapitiya, C., Calacanis, J., Sacks, D., Friedberg, D. (hosts), Altman, S. (guest). "In Conversation with Sam Altman". All-In Podcast, 10 May 2024.

## One-line summary

In an informal panel setting, Altman floats "universal basic compute" and repeats his call for an international agency to govern frontier AI.

## Key claims

- Altman expects continuous, incremental model improvement rather than discrete leaps.
- OpenAI's mission is to build toward AGI and broadly distribute its benefits.
- Altman anticipates an eventual international agency governing frontier AI systems capable of global harm.
- He states personal wealth is not his primary motivation for running OpenAI.
- He wants AI assistants to behave like a trusted senior employee, including pushing back on requests.
- He floats "universal basic compute" as a benefits-sharing model.

## Excerpts

> "The whole system just gets better and better fairly continuously...that's both a better technological direction."
> ~ On continuous improvement

> "I think there's great roles for both...our mission is to build towards AGI and figure out how to broadly distribute its benefits."
> ~ On open-source strategy

> "I think there will come a time...where frontier AI systems are capable of causing significant global harm...some sort of international agency."
> ~ On AI safety and regulation

> "I don't really need more money. Like, and I think people think it's a little bit of an ulterior motive."
> ~ On personal motivation

> "Universal basic compute...everybody gets like a slice of GPT-7's compute and they can use it, they can resell it."
> ~ On universal basic income research

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Extends the equity-fund idea from Moore's Law for Everything into a compute-denominated form.
- Repeats the international-agency proposal first raised in the 2023 Senate testimony.
- Downplays personal financial motive to a friendly, informal panel, contrasting his more guarded congressional register.
