# Dwarkesh Patel interview — Dario Amodei

## Citation

Patel, D., & Amodei, D. (2024). *Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO) - $10 Billion Models, OpenAI, Scaling, & AGI in 2 years*. Dwarkesh Podcast.

## One-line summary

Two-hour interview anchored on timelines, the structure of the alignment problem, mechanistic interpretability as "X-ray", and why Amodei resists probability distributions on alignment success.

## Key claims

- Human-level capability "could happen in two or three years" absent safety-driven slowdown.
- Alignment is not a Riemann-hypothesis-style problem to be cracked. It is statistical and control-shaped. Neither "aligned by default" nor "doomed by default".
- Juggling metaphor: today we juggle three balls; scaling without method development means dropping five. Build repertoire over time.
- Mechanistic interpretability as extended test set and "X-ray" of the model; measures whether internal plans match external outputs.
- Refuses to state a probability distribution over alignment difficulty tiers. More interested in what evidence shifts mass between tiers.
- Places "substantial probability mass" on disaster occurring "in a completely different way than anyone had anticipated".
- Current systems cannot stop a sufficiently powerful misaligned model; capability will reach that threshold.

## Excerpts

> "I think that could happen in two or three years. The main thing that would stop it would be if we hit certain safety thresholds."

> "I don't think we're aligned by default, I don't think we're doomed by default and have some problem we need to solve. It has some kind of different character."

> "Right now if I try and juggle five balls or something, I can juggle three balls, I actually can, but I can't juggle five balls at all."

> "I have a substantial probability mass on - this all goes wrong, it's a complete disaster, but in a completely different way than anyone had anticipated it would."

> "Mechanistic interpretability is the only thing that even in principle is the thing where it's more like an X-ray of the model than modification of the model."
