# Read the full transcript of our interview with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei (2026)

## Citation

Kent, J. L. "Read the full transcript of our interview with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei" (interview with Dario Amodei). CBS News, 28 February 2026. https://www.cbsnews.com/news/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-full-transcript/.

## One-line summary

Amodei explains Anthropic's dispute with the US Department of War, saying the company accepts nearly all military uses but objects to two, and defends the stance as patriotic.

## Key claims

- Anthropic says it has been the most forward-leaning AI company in working with the US government and military.
- Anthropic accepts 98% or 99% of the use cases the Department of War wants, with two exceptions.
- The Department gave an ultimatum: agree in three days or face a supply chain risk designation or the Defense Production Act.
- Amodei frames AI as on an exponential trend, with compute doubling every four months.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a habit of anchoring a policy dispute in the exponential trend, so that urgency justifies firm limits.
- Suggests he casts safety restrictions as loyalty to the country rather than opposition to it.
