## One-line summary

At Davos, Amodei argues for a balanced stance on AI, predicts high growth alongside high unemployment, and says interpretability holds the key to safe models.

## Key claims

- Anthropic has tried to hold a consistent position that there is balance between benefits and risks.
- He expects very high GDP growth and potentially very high unemployment and inequality.
- He credits Anthropic's early focus on enterprise over consumer as a good choice.
- He is convinced that looking inside models is the key to making them safe and controllable.

## Excerpts

> "And I think throughout, throughout all of this, the approach that I have tried to take and the approach that Anthropic has tried to take is, is, is one of constancy of saying that there is balance here."
> ~ WEF Davos interview, 20 January 2026, 02:41-03:13

> "My view is the signature of this technology is it's going to take us to a world where we have very high GDP growth and potentially also very high unemployment and inequality."
> ~ WEF Davos interview, 20 January 2026, 04:01-04:36

> "I think one of the good choices we made early was to be a company that is focused on enterprise rather than consumer."
> ~ WEF Davos interview, 20 January 2026, 11:27-11:59

> "The science of looking inside the AI models, I am convinced that this ultimately holds the key to making the model safe and controllable, because it's the only ground truth we have."
> ~ WEF Davos interview, 20 January 2026, 26:07-26:37

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Suggests he pairs optimism about growth with open acknowledgement of distributional harm.
- Reads as a conviction that safety is an empirical science grounded in model internals.
