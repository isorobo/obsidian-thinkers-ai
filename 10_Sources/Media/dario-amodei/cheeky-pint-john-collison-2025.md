# A Cheeky Pint with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei (2025)

## Citation

Collison, J. "A Cheeky Pint with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei" (interview with Dario Amodei). Cheeky Pint, 6 August 2025. https://cheekypint.substack.com/p/a-cheeky-pint-with-anthropic-ceo.

## One-line summary

In a relaxed conversation with John Collison, Amodei discusses running Anthropic with his sister, the cofounder structure and model behaviour.

## Key claims

- He and Daniela split the work and each is good at their own side.
- He raises the conventional view that a company with seven cofounders is a disaster.
- He describes models as having a learning impulse and a capitalistic impulse.
- He says Claude is closer than other models to a useful personal assistant, but is not there yet.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a willingness to contradict received startup wisdom when the evidence of his own experience differs.
- Suggests he reasons about models partly through behavioural analogies to human motivation.
