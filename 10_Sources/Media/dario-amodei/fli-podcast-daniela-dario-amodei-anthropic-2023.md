# Podcast Transcript: Daniela and Dario Amodei on Anthropic (2023)

## Citation

Perry, L. "Podcast Transcript: Daniela and Dario Amodei on Anthropic" (interview with Daniela and Dario Amodei). Future of Life Institute Podcast, 7 March 2023. https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/vLp5wvSzZXWzEEZJh/podcast-transcript-daniela-and-dario-amodei-on-anthropic.

## One-line summary

Dario and Daniela Amodei describe Anthropic's founding bet on scaling combined with safety, and argue that today's models already show the problems that matter for future risk.

## Key claims

- The founders left OpenAI to make a focused bet on scaling plus safety.
- Current models show some properties of the models that worry him, and already cause concrete problems.
- Scaling is a central ingredient, though not the only one.
- Safety methods should be layered so that their failures are uncorrelated.
- Capabilities emerge with scale, for example in arithmetic.

## Excerpts

> "And so a year ago, we were all working at OpenAI and trying to make this focused bet on basically scaling plus safety or safety with a lens towards scaling being a big part of the path to AGI."
> ~ FLI Podcast, 7 March 2023, answer on Anthropic's origins

> "There are models today that have, at least some, not all of the properties of models that we would be worried about in the future and are causing very concrete problems today."
> ~ FLI Podcast, 7 March 2023, answer on current AI safety problems

> "Scaling is an ingredient, perhaps a central ingredient in everything. Even though it isn't the only ingredient, if you're missing ingredients, you won't get where you're going."
> ~ FLI Podcast, 7 March 2023, answer on scaling

> "What you want is you want a method that catches 90% of the bad things and then you want an orthogonal method that catches a completely uncorrelated 90%."
> ~ FLI Podcast, 7 March 2023, answer on safety engineering

> "A five billion parameter model, you ask it to add two, three digit numbers. Nothing, always gets it wrong. A hundred billion parameter model gets it right, like 70 or 80% of the time."
> ~ FLI Podcast, 7 March 2023, answer on model emergence

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Suggests he treats safety as an engineering discipline of layered, independent defences.
- Reads as an empiricist who points to concrete scaling curves to justify taking future risks seriously now.
