# Beyond Deep Learning (with Gary Marcus)

## Citation

Marcus, Gary, and Azeem Azhar. "Azeem's Picks: Beyond Deep Learning (with Gary Marcus)". Exponential View, Harvard Business Review, 10 May 2023. https://hbr.org/podcast/2023/05/azeems-picks-beyond-deep-learning-with-gary-marcus.

## One-line summary

Speaking with Azeem Azhar, Marcus argues that deep learning cannot incorporate explicit knowledge and that AI must return to reasoning and inference.

## Key claims

- No current system can read a news story and report who did what to whom, when, where, and why.
- AI should revisit early ideas about reasoning and inference.
- Deep learning's weakness is its inability to incorporate explicit knowledge directly.
- His rough AGI estimate is 30 to 50 years away.
- Progress depends on adding reasoning and explicit knowledge to learned systems.

## Excerpts

> "We still don't have any kind of a system that can read a news story and tell you who did what to whom, when, where, and why."
> ~ On deep learning's limitations

> "Deep learning's Achilles heel is it doesn't really have a way of directly incorporating explicit knowledge."
> ~ On common sense representation

> "We need to go back to some things that people thought about in the early history of AI, which is about reasoning and inference."
> ~ On reasoning requirements

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The news-story test gives a concrete benchmark for the comprehension he says systems lack.
- Calling for a return to reasoning and inference places him in continuity with classical AI.
- A 30-to-50-year AGI estimate marks his distance from near-term takeoff forecasts.
