# Is AI just all hype? w/Gary Marcus (2024)

## Citation

Sidhu, B. "Is AI just all hype? w/Gary Marcus" (interview with Gary Marcus). The TED AI Show, 9 July 2024. https://www.ted.com/pages/is-ai-just-all-hype-wgary-marcus-transcript.

## One-line summary

Marcus defines AGI as human-like flexibility, calls scaling laws empirical generalisations rather than laws, and says a true accelerationist should move away from LLMs.

## Key claims

- AGI means intelligence with the flexibility and generality of human intelligence.
- AI should be thought about as a cognitive psychologist would.
- Scaling "laws" are empirical generalisations and not laws.
- Accelerationists should divorce themselves from LLMs.
