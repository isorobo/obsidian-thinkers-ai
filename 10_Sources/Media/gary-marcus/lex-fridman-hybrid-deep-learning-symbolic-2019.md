# Gary Marcus: Toward a Hybrid of Deep Learning and Symbolic AI

## Citation

Marcus, Gary, and Lex Fridman. "Gary Marcus: Toward a Hybrid of Deep Learning and Symbolic AI". Lex Fridman Podcast, 3 October 2019. https://podcasts.happyscribe.com/lex-fridman-podcast-artificial-intelligence-ai/gary-marcus-toward-a-hybrid-of-deep-learning-and-symbolic-ai.

## One-line summary

In conversation with Lex Fridman, Marcus argues that deep learning cannot extract abstract concepts and that AI needs explicit symbol representation.

## Key claims

- Deep learning systems do not genuinely manipulate algebra-like variables the way humans do.
- He has never seen a deep learning system extract a true abstract concept.
- AI needs machinery that represents symbols, against the view that symbolic AI is obsolete.
- Networks can fudge particular cases without genuine understanding.
- The path forward combines neural and symbolic methods rather than abandoning either.

## Excerpts

> "Deep learning systems kind of emulate that, but they don't actually do it."
> ~ On algebra-like operations over variables

> "I've never seen a deep learning system really extract an abstract concept."
> ~ On the limits of deep learning

> "We really do need to have the stuff that represents symbols."
> ~ Responding to the claim that symbolic AI is obsolete

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The abstraction test he applies to deep learning matches the few-shot benchmark in his 2018 critique.
- Insisting on symbol representation against Hinton restates the divide that defines his public work.
- The conversational format shows the same argument he later formalises in the 2020 robust-AI paper.
