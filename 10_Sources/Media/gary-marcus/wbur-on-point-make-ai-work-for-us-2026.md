# How to make AI work for us (2026)

## Citation

Becker, D. "How to make AI work for us" (interview with Gary Marcus). WBUR On Point, 30 March 2026. https://www.wbur.org/onpoint/2026/03/30/how-to-make-ai-work-for-us.

## One-line summary

Marcus says the risks in his book have materialised, worries about critical thinking in schools, and sees both technical and political-economic problems.

## Key claims

- The warnings in his book have materialised.
- Critical thinking is disappearing from high school and college.
- He still wants AI to succeed and sees technical and political-economic problems.
- Consumers are beginning to boycott OpenAI.

## Excerpts

> "They've materialized. The book was, in a certain way, speculative. I was trying to read the writing on the wall and warn people about it."
> ~ On Taming Silicon Valley

> "There's basically no critical thinking in high school and college anymore. And that's going to be bad for all of society regardless of what you do and whether you have kids and so forth."
> ~ On education

> "I really want AI to succeed. I still think it's possible, but I think we have two problems now. One's technical and one's kind of political economics."
> ~ On the state of AI

> "What I'm increasingly starting to see regular people do is boycott. So a lot of people are boycotting OpenAI now. I think the number's up to 5 million or maybe more."
> ~ On public response

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a framing of AI problems as both technical and political, which mirrors his book.
- Suggests he looks to consumer pressure as a lever when regulation stalls.
