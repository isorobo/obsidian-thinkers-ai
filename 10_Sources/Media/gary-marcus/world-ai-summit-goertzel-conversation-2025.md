# Dr. Ben Goertzel's Conversation with Dr. Gary Marcus at the World AI Summit 2025 (2025)

## Citation

Goertzel, B. "Dr. Ben Goertzel's Conversation with Dr. Gary Marcus at the World AI Summit 2025" (conversation with Gary Marcus). World AI Summit 2025, 4 September 2025. https://singularitynet.io/dr-ben-goertzels-conversation-with-dr-gary-marcus-at-the-world-ai-summit-2025/.

## One-line summary

Marcus tells Goertzel that current AI is morally and technically inadequate, that language models cannot reliably follow rules, and that systems need formal representations.

## Key claims

- Current AI is morally and technically inadequate.
- Models cannot be given simple constraints and be expected to follow them.
- Models still make illegal chess moves.
- Systems need places for formal representations from computer science.

## Excerpts

> "I have a phrase I like, which is that AI, current AI, is morally and technically inadequate."
> ~ Opening section

> "You can't give it simple constraints to follow, like 'don't tell people how to make biological weapons' and expect that it will actually follow them."
> ~ Limitations section

> "They still make illegal moves. That's not subtle. That is a failure to adopt an explicit set of rules."
> ~ Limitations section

> "There have to be some places in the system where you can have all the formal representations that we know and love from computer science."
> ~ Knowledge representation section

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as an argument from engineering reliability: rule-following failures are treated as evidence of an architectural gap.
- Suggests he pairs a technical critique with a moral one.
