# Intelligent Machines 809 (2025)

## Citation

Laporte, L., Martineau, P. and Jarvis, J. "Intelligent Machines 809" (interview with Gary Marcus). Intelligent Machines (TWiT) #809, 5 March 2025. https://twit.tv/posts/transcripts/intelligent-machines-809-transcript.

## One-line summary

Marcus describes himself as an AI enthusiast who dislikes how it is being done, doubts extinction risk, and warns that demos are easy while delivery is hard.

## Key claims

- He loves AI but not the way it is happening now, and accepts the label contrarian.
- He thinks AI annihilating humanity is unlikely.
- A demo of anything is possible with enough data.
- Delivering on the sold dream probably needs something like AGI.
