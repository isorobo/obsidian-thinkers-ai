# How Misalignment Could Lead to Takeover (2023)

## Citation

Christiano, P. "How Misalignment Could Lead to Takeover". San Francisco Alignment Workshop, 26 February 2023. https://far.ai/events/sessions/paul-christiano-how-misalignment-could-lead-to-takeover.

## One-line summary

Christiano walks through how misaligned AI systems could end in total human disempowerment, which he calls takeover for short, via two failure modes.

## Key claims

- The talk covers how misalignment ultimately leads to total human disempowerment.
- A world with many AI systems can become hard for humans to understand.
- He thinks it quite likely that AI systems collectively disempowering humanity would succeed.
- He presents two failure modes and factors that could move the world between equilibria.
