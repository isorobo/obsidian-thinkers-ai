# Paul Christiano: Preventing AI Takeover

## Citation

Christiano, Paul, and Dwarkesh Patel. "Paul Christiano: Preventing AI Takeover". The Dwarkesh Podcast, 31 October 2023. https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/paul-christiano.

## One-line summary

Christiano gives probabilities for transformative AI, sketches a future of continued human competition aided by cognitive tools, and warns how fast misaligned systems could cause harm.

## Key claims

- He puts roughly a 15% chance of transformative capability by 2030 and 40% by 2040.
- His median future keeps economic and military competition among groups of humans.
- He prefers building cognitive tools that help humans without themselves being moral entities.
- Failing AI systems could do large harm very quickly once relied upon.
- Preventing takeover is about keeping humans in control of consequential decisions.

## Excerpts

> "15% chance by 2030 and like 40% chance by 2040."
> ~ On AI capability timelines

> "I'm imagining my typical future has sort of continuing economic and military competition amongst groups of humans."
> ~ On post-AGI civilisation

> "If you're building cognitive tools that help humans do things without themselves being like moral entities. That's like what you would prefer."
> ~ On the preferred alignment approach

> "It's quite easy to be in a position where if AI systems started failing, they could do a lot of harm very quickly."
> ~ On catastrophic failure

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Christiano quantifies uncertainty with explicit probabilities rather than rhetoric, a signature of his public reasoning.
- His preference for non-agentic cognitive tools follows from the influence-seeking worry in his essays.
- The picture of continued human competition shows he expects a contested transition, not a single decisive event.
