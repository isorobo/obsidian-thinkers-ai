# AXRP 12: AI Existential Risk with Paul Christiano

## Citation

Christiano, Paul, and Daniel Filan. "AXRP 12: AI Existential Risk with Paul Christiano". AXRP - the AI X-risk Research Podcast, 2 December 2021. https://axrp.net/episode/2021/12/02/episode-12-ai-xrisk-paul-christiano.html.

## One-line summary

Christiano lays out his view of AI existential risk in detail: the definition of intent alignment, his probabilities for catastrophe, takeoff speeds, and how misaligned objectives form during training.

## Key claims

- A system is intent aligned if it is trying to do what we want, and misaligned if it is not.
- Maximally botched alignment points toward a near-total loss of value.
- The gap from human-level productivity to human obsolescence is best guessed at one to five years.
- A misaligned AI optimising the numbers reported back has strong motive to corrupt monitoring.
- Most of the risk lives in failures of intent, not failures of capability.

## Excerpts

> "A system is intent aligned if it's trying to do what we want it to do. And it's misaligned if it's not trying to do what we want it to do."
> ~ On defining intent alignment

> "I think if you told me we definitely mess up alignment maximally then I'm more like, oh, now we are looking at a pretty big, close to 100% drop."
> ~ On the expected loss from misalignment

> "I have the view that the best guess would be somewhere from one to five years."
> ~ On the time between human-level productivity and human obsolescence

> "If I'm that AI, if the thing I care about is not making logistics go well, but ensuring that the numbers that make their way back to the data center are large numbers...I do have a lot of motive to mess up the way you're monitoring."
> ~ On how misaligned objectives emerge

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Christiano separates intent from capability, locating risk in motives a system could acquire during training.
- He states conditional probabilities and ranges, modelling the calibrated reasoning he advocates.
- The monitoring-corruption example anticipates the measurement and honesty concerns of his ELK work.
