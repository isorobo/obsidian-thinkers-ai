# Five Questions for Erik Brynjolfsson (2017)

## Citation

Reese, H. "Five Questions for Erik Brynjolfsson" (interview with Erik Brynjolfsson). Undark, 31 August 2017. https://undark.org/article/five-questions-ai-erik-brynjolfsson/.

## One-line summary

In a short 2017 interview Brynjolfsson links machine learning, platform companies and data-driven management to a coming economic and social shift.

## Key claims

- Platform companies are spreading across the economy.
- Machines are learning the right steps on their own.
- Data-driven firms are about 5 percent more productive.
- More powerful AI will lead to profound economic and social change.

## Excerpts

> "Five of the biggest companies are now platform companies, but it's happening all over the economy."
> ~ Brynjolfsson, Undark, 31 August 2017

> "Machines are learning how to figure out the right steps on their own."
> ~ Brynjolfsson, Undark, 31 August 2017

> "The evidence shows that companies that adopt this data-driven approach are about 5 percent more productive."
> ~ Brynjolfsson, Undark, 31 August 2017

> "As artificial intelligence becomes more powerful, it will inevitably lead to profound changes in our economy and society."
> ~ Brynjolfsson, Undark, 31 August 2017

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- He cites measured productivity effects rather than anecdotes, which suggests an empirical habit of argument.
- The early focus on machines learning on their own reads as a thread that continues into his later agent-era writing.
