# The Key to Growth? Race with the Machines

## Citation

Brynjolfsson, Erik. "The Key to Growth? Race with the Machines". TED2013, Long Beach, California, February 2013. https://www.ted.com/talks/erik_brynjolfsson_the_key_to_growth_race_with_the_machines.

## One-line summary

Brynjolfsson argues that current economic troubles are the growing pains of a new machine age and that the path forward is for humans to race with machines rather than against them.

## Key claims

- Current economic troubles are misdiagnosed as the end of innovation; they are growing pains of a new machine age.
- Computers improve faster than any prior technology, and the new machine age will take a century to play out.
- Workers who race against machines lose; the answer is not to slow technology.
- Human-machine collaboration outperforms either humans or machines alone.

## Excerpts

> "Racing with the machine beats racing the machine."
> ~ Race with the Machines, TED2013

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The "race with the machine" slogan is the popular root of his later complementarity and Turing Trap arguments.
- His refusal to slow technology, paired with a call to change how it is used, fixes a stance he holds for the next decade.
- Co-crediting Andrew McAfee marks the partnership behind The Second Machine Age and Machine, Platform, Crowd.
