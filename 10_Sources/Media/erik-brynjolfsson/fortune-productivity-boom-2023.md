# Top AI Economist Says It Will Create a Productivity Boom

## Citation

Colvin, Geoff. "Top AI Economist Says It Will Create a Productivity Boom". Fortune, 26 September 2023. https://fortune.com/2023/09/26/ai-economist-erik-brynjolfsson-productivity-boom-labor/.

## One-line summary

In an interview with Fortune, Brynjolfsson urges firms to augment rather than mimic humans and to embrace generative AI to unlock far greater value.

## Key claims

- AI creates the most value when it augments humans rather than mimicking them.
- Augmentation generates more value than merely human-like AI.
- Firms should embrace the technology rather than resist it.
- Every company should help employees use AI more, not less.

## Excerpts

> "augmentation creates new capabilities and new products and services, ultimately generating far more value than merely human-like AI"
> ~ Fortune interview, 26 September 2023

> "You need to embrace this technology and not resist it"
> ~ Fortune interview, 26 September 2023

> "This is a transformative technology. Every company should be working really hard to figure out how its employees can use it more, not less"
> ~ Fortune interview, 26 September 2023

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The interview translates his academic augmentation thesis into direct managerial advice.
- Framing AI as value-creating, not merely cost-saving, restates the upswing of his productivity J-curve.
- His "embrace, do not resist" stance matches the TED-era refusal to slow technology.
