# Godfather of AI: I Tried to Warn Them, But We've Already Lost Control (2025)

## Citation

Bartlett, Steven. "Godfather of AI: I Tried to Warn Them, But We've Already Lost Control! Geoffrey Hinton". The Diary of a CEO, 16 June 2025. Transcript via Singju Post. https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-godfather-of-ai-i-tried-to-warn-them-but-weve-already-lost-control/.

## One-line summary

Hinton names warning the public as his mission, places superintelligence within twenty years or less, predicts severe job losses, and argues that systems more intelligent than us will require a form of world government.

## Key claims

- His main mission now is to warn people how dangerous AI could be.
- Few people once believed artificial neural networks could be made to work.
- The existential threat is one we have no idea how to deal with.
- Superintelligence might arrive in about twenty years or even less.
- Call-centre work is among the jobs most exposed to displacement.
- Producing systems more intelligent than us calls for a kind of world government.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Mission framing: he casts his late career as public warning, a deliberate turn from research to advocacy.
- Compressed timelines: he places superintelligence within two decades or less, far sooner than his earlier estimates.
- Concrete labour impact: he names specific exposed jobs rather than abstract disruption.
- Governance scale: he reaches for world government, treating the risk as beyond any single state.
