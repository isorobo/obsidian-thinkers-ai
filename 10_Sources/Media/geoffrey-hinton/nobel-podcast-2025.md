# Geoffrey Hinton Nobel Prize Podcast (2025)

## Citation

Smith, Adam, and Karin Svensson. "Geoffrey Hinton: Podcast". NobelPrize.org, 15 May 2025. https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2024/hinton/podcast/.

## One-line summary

In a long-form Nobel podcast, Hinton reflects on his restless education, his theory that memory is plausible reconstruction, and his view that machine consciousness and the control problem are real and unavoidable.

## Key claims

- His Cambridge studies jumped between subjects with no coherent plan.
- Memory is reconstruction: people make up a plausible story rather than replay a record.
- There is no reason these systems will not be conscious.
- The real question is whether we can stop more intelligent systems from wanting to take over.
- AI will not be stopped because the profits at stake are too large.

## Excerpts

> "If you look at my academic history at Cambridge, I switched subjects every year and it didn't make any sense at all."
~ NobelPrize.org podcast, 15 May 2025

> "When we remember, what we're doing is just making up a story that sounds plausible to us. That's what memories are."
~ NobelPrize.org podcast, 15 May 2025

> "There's no reason why these things won't be conscious."
~ NobelPrize.org podcast, 15 May 2025

> "The real question is not will they get more intelligent than us, but if they're more intelligent than us, will we have a way of making sure they don't want to take over?"
~ NobelPrize.org podcast, 15 May 2025

> "We are not going to stop AI...There's too many profits to be made."
~ NobelPrize.org podcast, 15 May 2025

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Memory as fabrication: he treats human recall as reconstruction, the same lens he applies to model confabulation.
- Consciousness without mysticism: he asserts machine consciousness is plausible, refusing to treat it as off-limits.
- Control framing: he reframes the danger from raw intelligence to the goal of avoiding takeover.
- Economic fatalism: he judges a pause unrealistic because the incentives to build are overwhelming.
