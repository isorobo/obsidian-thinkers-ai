# Living with Alien Beings (2026 Ewan Lecture) (2026)

## Citation

Hinton, G. "Living with Alien Beings" (2026 Ewan Lecture). Queen's University, Ontario, Arthur B. McDonald Canadian Astroparticle Physics Research Institute, 29 January 2026. https://singjupost.com/2026-ewan-lecture-by-prof-geoffrey-hinton-living-with-alien-beings/. ## One-line summary

Hinton argues that large language models understand language much as people do and that a multimodal chatbot already has subjective experience.

## Key claims

- Language models understand sentences in a way very similar to human understanding.
- Language is a general method of modelling things.
- Current models hold far more knowledge than any one person.
- Governments share an interest in staying in charge rather than letting AI take over.
- A multimodal chatbot already has subjective experience.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Hinton reads as treating machine understanding as continuous with human understanding, not a mimic of it.
- The appeal to shared state interests suggests he sees room for international cooperation on loss of control.
- Opening with a promise to convince the audience suggests he argues for machine consciousness as a thesis, not a speculation.
