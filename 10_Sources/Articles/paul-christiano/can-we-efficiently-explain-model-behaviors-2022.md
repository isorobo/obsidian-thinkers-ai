# Can we efficiently explain model behaviors? (2022)

## Citation

Christiano, P. "Can we efficiently explain model behaviors?". ARC Blog, 16 December 2022. https://www.alignment.org/blog/can-we-efficiently-explain-model-behaviors/.

## One-line summary

Christiano frames finding explanations of model behaviour as a search problem and argues that its tractability bears on both ELK and interpretability in general.

## Key claims

- The first of three steps is formalising probabilistic heuristic argument as an operationalisation of "explanation".
- Finding explanations is a relatively unambitious interpretability goal, so intractability would be an important obstacle to interpretability generally.
- The complexity of finding explanations is bounded by the complexity of finding and running the model.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a preference for stating the minimal formal sub-problem and asking whether it is computationally tractable.
- Suggests he treats a negative result as informative about the whole interpretability programme.
