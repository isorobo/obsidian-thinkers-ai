# Prizes for matrix completion problems (2023)

## Citation

Christiano, P. "Prizes for matrix completion problems". ARC Blog, 3 May 2023. https://www.alignment.org/blog/prize-for-matrix-completion-problems/.

## One-line summary

ARC offers bounties for two self-contained algorithmic questions that arose in its work on heuristic estimators.

## Key claims

- Two self-contained algorithmic questions came up in ARC's research.
- A $5k bounty applies to either, for an algorithm or a lower bound under a known hardness assumption.
- Winners may publish independently.
- ARC is trying to find efficient heuristic estimators as a formalisation of defeasible reasoning.

## Excerpts

> "Here are two self-contained algorithmic questions that have come up in our research."
> ~ ARC Blog, 3 May 2023, opening

> "We're offering a bounty of $5k for a solution to either of them - either an algorithm, or a lower bound under any hardness assumption that has appeared in the literature."
> ~ ARC Blog, 3 May 2023

> "Winners are welcome to publish solutions independently."
> ~ ARC Blog, 3 May 2023

> "ARC is trying to find efficient heuristic estimators as a formalization of defeasible reasoning about quantities like the variance of a neural network's output."
> ~ ARC Blog, 3 May 2023

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Suggests a habit of carving research into crisp, outsourceable sub-problems.
- Reads as openness to external contributors on core theory questions.
