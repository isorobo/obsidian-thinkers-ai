# Thoughts on responsible scaling policies and regulation (2023)

## Citation

Christiano, P. "Thoughts on responsible scaling policies and regulation". AI Alignment Forum, 24 October 2023. https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/dxgEaDrEBkkE96CXr/thoughts-on-responsible-scaling-policies-and-regulation.

## One-line summary

Christiano argues that good responsible scaling policies could cut risk sharply and support regulation, while stressing that voluntary commitments are no substitute for it.

## Key claims

- Sufficiently good RSPs could dramatically reduce risk, and early policies such as Anthropic's create urgency.
- Voluntary RSPs are not a substitute for regulation.
- The safest action in an ideal world would be pausing immediately, but current risk is low enough that continuing is defensible given a good detection plan.
- RSPs make effective regulation easier, and a very good RSP might cut risk perhaps tenfold.

## Excerpts

> "I think that sufficiently good responsible scaling policies could dramatically reduce risk, and that preliminary policies like Anthropic's RSP meaningfully reduce risk by creating urgency around key protective measures and increasing the probability of a pause if those measures can't be implemented quickly enough."
> ~ Alignment Forum post, 24 October 2023, opening summary

> "I don't think voluntary implementation of responsible scaling policies is a substitute for regulation. Voluntary commitments are unlikely to be universally adopted or to have adequate oversight, and I think the public should demand a higher degree of safety than AI developers are likely to voluntarily implement."
> ~ Alignment Forum post, 24 October 2023

> "I think the safest action in an ideal world would be pausing immediately until we were better prepared (though see the caveats in the next section). But the current level of risk is low enough that I think it is defensible for companies or countries to continue AI development if they have a sufficiently good plan for detecting and reacting to increasing risk."
> ~ Alignment Forum post, 24 October 2023

> "I believe that AI developers implementing RSPs will make it easier rather than harder to implement effective regulation."
> ~ Alignment Forum post, 24 October 2023

> "I believe that a very good RSP (of the kind I've been advocating for) could cut risk dramatically if implemented effectively, perhaps a 10x reduction."
> ~ Alignment Forum post, 24 October 2023

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a conditional, cost-benefit stance: pausing is ideal in principle but contingent policies are defensible now.
- Suggests he treats voluntary industry measures and regulation as complements and expects the first to build momentum for the second.
