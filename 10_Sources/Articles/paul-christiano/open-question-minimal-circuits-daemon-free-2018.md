# Open question: are minimal circuits daemon-free? (2018)

## Citation

Christiano, P. "Open question: are minimal circuits daemon-free?". AI Alignment Forum, 5 May 2018. https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/nyCHnY7T5PHPLjxmN/open-question-are-minimal-circuits-daemon-free.

## One-line summary

Christiano asks whether selecting the smallest circuit that solves a problem would avoid daemons, agents that solve the problem only instrumentally, and notes obstacles to a proof.

## Key claims

- He is convinced that daemons are a real problem for Solomonoff induction.
- He suspects daemons are not a problem if selection is only for computational efficiency.
- The first and most serious difficulty is understanding what is being discussed.
- A daemon may be cryptographically obfuscated in an extreme case.
