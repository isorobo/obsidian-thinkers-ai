# Returning to ARC (2026)

## Citation

Christiano, P. "Returning to ARC". AI Alignment Forum, 4 August 2026. https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/vLFh8HP3hyNy9MCwe/returning-to-arc.

## One-line summary

Christiano announces his return to lead ARC and argues that finding mechanistic explanations of neural networks is a needed backup if current alignment and control methods fail.

## Key claims

- Modern AI systems are not aligned with human intent, and more powerful models will likely take unintended actions to succeed.
- He guesses a 20-30% chance that existing alignment and control methods break down before broadly superhuman AI.
- ARC's core hypothesis is that ML systems differ only quantitatively in complexity from other computational systems.
- He gives ARC about a 10% chance of reaching its most ambitious goals in time.

## Excerpts

> "Modern AI systems are not aligned with human intent. We will likely train increasingly powerful models that take unintended actions in order to succeed at their task"
> ~ Returning to ARC, 4 August 2026, opening passage

> "If you made me guess I'd say that there's a 20-30% chance that existing methods for alignment and control break down before we reach broadly superhuman AI."
> ~ Returning to ARC, 4 August 2026

> "ARC's core hypothesis is that machine learning systems are not qualitatively different from other computational systems, they are just quantitatively more complex."
> ~ Returning to ARC, 4 August 2026

> "I think ARC has a ~10% chance of achieving our most ambitious goals before broadly superhuman AI obsoletes our labor."
> ~ Returning to ARC, 4 August 2026

> "Because we don't understand why a neural network works, we have no idea what changes to the input distribution might cause it to stop working."
> ~ Returning to ARC, 4 August 2026

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as calibrated and explicit about probabilities, including pessimism about his own organisation's odds.
- Suggests a view of ML systems as ordinary computations that admit explanation, which motivates the interpretability-flavoured agenda.
