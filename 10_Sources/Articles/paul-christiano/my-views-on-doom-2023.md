# My views on "doom"

## Citation

Christiano, Paul. "My views on 'doom'". LessWrong, 27 April 2023. https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/xWMqsvHapP3nwdSW8/my-views-on-doom.

## One-line summary

Christiano decomposes the question of AI catastrophe into separate probabilities, distinguishing extinction from bad futures, and puts AI takeover at 22% and irreversible harm within a decade at 46%.

## Key claims

- Extinction risk (dying) and existential risk (a bad future) are distinct outcomes that demand separate estimates.
- AI takeover names a world governed by AI systems that do not share human values.
- He assigns AI takeover a probability of 22%.
- He puts the chance most humans die within 10 years of powerful AI at 20%.
- He puts the chance humanity irreversibly damages its future within 10 years of powerful AI at 46%.

## Excerpts

> "I'm often asked: 'what's the probability of a really bad outcome from AI?'"
> ~ Opening framing

> "One distinction is between dying ('extinction' risk) and having a bad future ('existential' risk)."
> ~ Two distinctions section

> "AI takeover: an outcome where the world is governed by AI systems, and we weren't able to build AI systems who share our values or care a lot about helping us."
> ~ Two distinctions section

> "Probability of an AI takeover: 22%"
> ~ Probability estimates

> "Probability that humanity has somehow irreversibly messed up our future within 10 years of building powerful AI: 46%"
> ~ Probability estimates

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Christiano refuses a single doom number; he splits the question into well-defined sub-events first.
- He separates extinction from value loss, treating a surviving but disempowered humanity as its own failure mode.
- His estimates sit well below pure-pessimist forecasts yet above complacency, matching his iterate-and-fix stance.
