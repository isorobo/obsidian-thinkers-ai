# AI alignment landscape

## Citation

Christiano, Paul. "AI alignment landscape". AI Alignment Forum, 13 October 2019. https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/zQgqAc9nQETjFjawJ/ai-alignment-landscape.

## One-line summary

Christiano charts how intent alignment sits inside the broader project of making AI go well, decomposing the problem into alignment, competence, and coping with impacts.

## Key claims

- Intent alignment fits inside the broader project of making AI go well.
- The problem decomposes into alignment, competence, and coping with the impacts of AI.
- Intent-aligned systems are powerful AI that are trying to do what we want.
- His own work concentrates on a few highlighted parts of the landscape.
- The map orients researchers to where effort is most needed.

- Christiano builds a shared map of the field, a recurring move that lets others locate their work against his.
- Decomposing into alignment, competence, and impacts shows his habit of carving a problem before solving it.
- Naming where he personally focuses signals which sub-problems he judges most tractable.
