# Matrix completion prize results (2023)

## Citation

Christiano, P. "Matrix completion prize results". ARC Blog, 20 December 2023. https://www.alignment.org/blog/matrix-completion-prize-results/.

## One-line summary

ARC reports that its matrix completion prize produced useful submissions but no complete solutions, and that the problems are likely hard.

## Key claims

- The prize drew useful submissions but no complete solutions.
- The main update is that the problems are hard with probably no overlooked simple solution.
- ARC spent the year refining goals for heuristic estimators.
- ARC expects to relax the matrix completion problems in a similar way.

## Excerpts

> "We received a number of submissions we considered useful, but not any complete solutions."
> ~ ARC Blog, 20 December 2023

> "Our main update from running this prize is that these problems are hard and there's probably not a simple solution we are overlooking."
> ~ ARC Blog, 20 December 2023

> "One of ARC's main activities this year has been refining our goals for heuristic estimators by finding algorithms, finding evidence for hardness, and clarifying what properties are actually needed for our desired alignment applications."
> ~ ARC Blog, 20 December 2023

> "We now expect to make a similar relaxation for these matrix completion problems."
> ~ ARC Blog, 20 December 2023

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a willingness to report null results and revise problem statements rather than defend them.
- Suggests ARC's method is iterative narrowing of the target to what alignment applications need.
