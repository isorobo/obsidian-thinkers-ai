# AMA: Paul Christiano, alignment researcher

## Citation

Christiano, Paul. "AMA: Paul Christiano, alignment researcher". AI Alignment Forum, 28 April 2021. https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/7qhtuQLCCvmwCPfXK/ama-paul-christiano-alignment-researcher.

## One-line summary

In an open question session Christiano explains his move toward conceptual alignment work at ARC, his taste for well-posed problems, and the value of strong applied alignment teams.

## Key claims

- He left OpenAI to work on more conceptual and theoretical alignment problems.
- He prefers well-posed problems, where theorems can be proved.
- Alignment improves when strong teams apply best practices to state-of-the-art models.
- The unrestricted adversarial examples challenge is a research direction he values.
- His agenda mixes theoretical foundations with applied safety practice.

## Excerpts

> "My own departure was driven largely by my desire to work on more conceptual/theoretical issues in alignment."
> ~ On leaving OpenAI for ARC

> "I like working on well-posed problems, and proving theorems about well-posed problems are particularly great."
> ~ On his research taste

> "I think alignment is a lot better if there are strong teams trying to apply best practices to align state of the art models."
> ~ On applied alignment teams

> "I really like the idea of the unrestricted adversarial examples challenge. I wish that the contest was more of a thing."
> ~ On a favoured research direction

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The move to conceptual work explains the founding of ARC and the turn toward ELK.
- His taste for well-posed problems and theorems marks the theoretical-computer-science streak in his approach.
- Valuing strong applied teams shows he sees theory and practice as complementary, not rival.
