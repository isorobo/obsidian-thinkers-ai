# Machines of Mind: The Case for an AI-Powered Productivity Boom

## Citation

Baily, Martin Neil, Erik Brynjolfsson, and Anton Korinek. "Machines of Mind: The Case for an AI-Powered Productivity Boom". Brookings Institution, 10 May 2023. https://www.brookings.edu/articles/machines-of-mind-the-case-for-an-ai-powered-productivity-boom/.

## One-line summary

The authors argue that large language models will both lift worker productivity and speed innovation, laying the foundation for an acceleration in economic growth.

## Key claims

- Large language models make workers more productive and raise the rate of innovation.
- Productivity growth, output per hour worked, is the primary determinant of long-term prosperity.
- Early estimates suggest large language models could affect 80% of the United States workforce in some form.
- Faster productivity growth can mitigate many social challenges, from living standards to healthcare.

## Excerpts

> "Large language models such as ChatGPT are emerging as powerful tools that not only make workers more productive but also increase the rate of innovation, laying the foundation for a significant acceleration in economic growth."
> ~ Introduction

> "The primary determinant of our long-term prosperity and welfare is the rate of productivity growth: the amount of output created per hour worked."
> ~ Section "The Power of Productivity Growth"

> "Faster productivity growth is an elixir that can solve or mitigate many of our society's challenges, from raising living standards and addressing poverty to providing healthcare for all."
> ~ Conclusion

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The essay states Brynjolfsson's optimistic case for AI, framing the technology as a driver of growth rather than a threat, the upswing of his J-curve.
- Anchoring the argument in productivity growth as the master variable reflects his consistent macroeconomic lens on technological change.
- Writing for a policy audience at Brookings shows his intent to shape institutional response, not only academic debate.
