# AI Changed Work Forever in 2025 (2026)

## Citation

Brynjolfsson, E. "AI Changed Work Forever in 2025". TIME, 2 January 2026. https://time.com/7342494/ai-changed-work-forever/.

## One-line summary

Brynjolfsson argues that AI is commoditising execution, shifting value to asking questions and evaluating results, and that the key issue by 2050 is who decides what AI does.

## Key claims

- AI is becoming very good at execution.
- As execution is commoditised, value moves to questions and evaluation.
- Orchestrating AI agents sharply lowers the cost of trying new things.
- By 2050 people will command large agent workforces, and control of AI's direction is the central question.

## Excerpts

> "By 2050, most people will command workforces larger than the biggest multinational corporations of today."
> ~ Opening vision, TIME, 2 January 2026

> "AI is getting astonishingly good at Part 2: Execution."
> ~ TIME, 2 January 2026

> "As execution becomes commoditized, the bottleneck - and the value - shifts to asking the right questions and evaluating results."
> ~ TIME, 2 January 2026 (dashes in the original replaced with hyphens)

> "When anyone with a creative spark can orchestrate a cloud of AI agents to build prototypes, analyze markets, and test hypotheses, the cost of trying something new plummets."
> ~ TIME, 2 January 2026

> "By 2050, the most important question about AI will not be what it can do, but who gets to decide what it does."
> ~ Closing, TIME, 2 January 2026

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The forward-dated framing (2050) suggests he argues from scenarios and design choices rather than point forecasts.
- Ending on who decides reads as moving from a productivity case to a governance case.
