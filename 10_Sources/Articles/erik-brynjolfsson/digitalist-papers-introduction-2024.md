# The Digitalist Papers, Introduction: Artificial Intelligence and Democracy in America

## Citation

Brynjolfsson, Erik, Alex Pentland, Nathaniel Persily, Condoleezza Rice, and Angela Aristidou. "Introduction: Artificial Intelligence and Democracy in America". In The Digitalist Papers. Stanford Digital Economy Lab, 2024. https://www.digitalistpapers.com/essays/introduction.

## One-line summary

The introduction frames AI as a force on a par with the upheavals that produced the Federalist Papers and calls for institutional reinvention to make democracy more transparent and accountable.

## Key claims

- The present moment, like the late 18th century, demands the rebuilding or reinvention of institutions.
- The Digitalist Papers gather experts across economics, law, technology, management, and political science.
- Uncertain times require a diverse range of perspectives rather than a single prescription.
- AI can be leveraged to design more transparent, accountable institutions, not only to threaten them.

## Excerpts

> "We, too, stand at technological, economic, and political crossroads that demand creative rebuilding or reinvention of new institutions."
> ~ Introduction

> "The Digitalist Papers series aims to bridge domains and disciplines by assembling experts from multiple fields-including economics, law, technology, management, and political science-alongside industry and civil society leaders."
> ~ Introduction

> "If powerful digital technologies, like AI, can profoundly amplify and affect political processes, we can also leverage them to design policies and institutions that are more transparent, accountable, and better suited to contemporary needs."
> ~ Introduction

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Extending his economic frame to democratic institutions shows that Brynjolfsson treats AI as a governance challenge, not only a labour one.
- The refusal to offer a single prescription mirrors his "fork in the road" stance: outcomes are designed through deliberation.
- Convening cross-disciplinary authors reflects his role as institution-builder at the Digital Economy Lab.
