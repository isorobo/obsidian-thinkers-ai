# Reflections

## Citation

Altman, Sam. "Reflections". Personal blog, 5 January 2025. https://blog.samaltman.com/reflections.

## One-line summary

Looking back on 2024, Altman states OpenAI now knows how to build AGI and is turning its aim toward superintelligence.

## Key claims

- OpenAI was founded on the belief that AGI was possible and would be hugely impactful.
- ChatGPT's launch produced unprecedented growth across the company, the industry, and the world.
- Rapid scaling at OpenAI has been a messy, iterative process.
- OpenAI states it is now confident it knows how to build AGI as traditionally defined.
- OpenAI's next stated aim is superintelligence in the fuller sense of the term.
- Altman reflects on his November 2023 removal and reinstatement as a formative governance test.

## Excerpts

> "We started OpenAI almost nine years ago because we believed that AGI was possible, and that it could be the most impactful technology in human history."
> ~ Opening

> "The launch of ChatGPT kicked off a growth curve like nothing we have ever seen—in our company, our industry, and the world broadly."
> ~ On 2024 growth

> "Building up a company at such high velocity with so little training is a messy process. It's often two steps forward, one step back."
> ~ On scaling operations

> "We are now confident we know how to build AGI as we have traditionally understood it."
> ~ On capability

> "We are beginning to turn our aim beyond that, to superintelligence in the true sense of the word."
> ~ On the next goal

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Frames OpenAI's mission in escalating terms, moving the goalpost from AGI to superintelligence.
- Uses personal narrative about the 2023 crisis to reinforce loyalty and governance lessons in public.
- Signals that internal capability confidence, not external benchmarks, drives OpenAI's roadmap statements.
