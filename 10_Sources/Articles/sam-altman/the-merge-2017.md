# The Merge

## Citation

Altman, Sam. "The Merge". Personal blog, 7 December 2017. https://blog.samaltman.com/the-merge.

## One-line summary

Altman argues humans have already begun merging with machines through dependence on algorithms, and that a deliberate, well-designed merge is humanity's best survival strategy.

## Key claims

- Silicon Valley regularly debates the year humans and machines will merge.
- Altman argues the merge already began through daily dependence on algorithms and devices.
- Complex algorithms driving daily life are no longer understood by any single person.
- Technological merger cannot be stopped once the laws of physics permit it.
- Humanity should design the merge as a team effort that protects everyone's well-being.
- Humans may become the first species to design their own descendants.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Treats human-machine merger as inevitable physics rather than a policy choice, years before AGI entered mainstream debate.
- Establishes an early version of the "one team" framing he later applies to global AI governance.
- Reveals a long-standing personal interest in the topic, later reflected in his co-founding of the brain-computer interface start-up Merge Labs.
