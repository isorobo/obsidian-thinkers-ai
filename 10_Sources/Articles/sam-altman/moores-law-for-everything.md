# Moore's Law for Everything

## Citation

Altman, Sam. "Moore's Law for Everything". Personal blog, 16 March 2021. https://moores.samaltman.com/.

## One-line summary

Altman argues AI-driven deflation will make goods and services radically cheaper, and proposes a capital tax that funds a citizen equity fund to share the gains.

## Key claims

- Software that can think and learn will take over more of the work people do today.
- Technological progress over the next 100 years will exceed everything achieved since humans controlled fire and invented the wheel.
- "Moore's Law for everything" should become the rallying cry for a generation priced out of what it wants.
- Capitalism rewards investment in capital, so wealth concentrates with asset owners rather than workers.
- Policy should shift taxation from labour toward capital and land as AI automates production.
- A nation-wide equity fund, funded by a tax on large companies and privately held land, should pay dividends to every citizen over eighteen.
- Falling costs, not rising wages, will drive most future gains in living standards.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Altman frames AI primarily as a deflationary force on the cost of goods, a theme he repeats in later essays.
- The proposed equity fund shows an early preference for redistribution through ownership rather than income transfers.
- Written twenty months before ChatGPT, the essay shows Altman forecasting mass AI impact well ahead of public awareness.
