# Personal Update: I've Joined Anthropic

## Citation

Karpathy, Andrej. "Personal update: I've joined Anthropic". X (formerly Twitter), 19 May 2026. https://x.com/karpathy/status/2056753169888334312.

## One-line summary

Karpathy announces he has paused Eureka Labs to join Anthropic's pre-training team, returning to frontier LLM research under team lead Nick Joseph.

## Key claims

- Karpathy joins Anthropic's pre-training team to help build large-scale training runs that give Claude its core capabilities.
- He frames the coming years at the frontier of LLMs as "especially formative", justifying a return to full-time research.
- The move pauses, rather than ends, Eureka Labs; he states he remains committed to education and intends to resume that work later.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Karpathy treats institutional moves as instrumental to research access, returning to a frontier lab once he judges the field to be entering a formative period.
- The statement's brevity matches his established preference for short, declarative public announcements over extended justification.
- Naming education as paused rather than abandoned preserves continuity across his Tesla, OpenAI, Eureka Labs, and Anthropic moves; each transition is framed as additive, not a rejection of prior work.
- The move returns him to a frontier lab, and to a competitor of the OpenAI where he began. The announcement itself draws no such contrast; this reading is inference.
