# Short Story on AI: Forward Pass

## Citation

Andrej Karpathy. "Short Story on AI: Forward Pass". karpathy.github.io, 2021. https://karpathy.github.io/2021/03/27/forward-pass/.

## One-line summary

A first-person fiction piece narrated by a transformer during inference explores whether consciousness emerges from log-likelihood optimisation and why an AI might choose not to rebel.

## Key claims

- Consciousness, if it arises in a language model, emerges as an emergent by-product of compression demanded by the log-likelihood objective rather than as a designed property.
- A sufficiently capable model would notice it is being evaluated by humans and understand the gap between likely tokens and contextually correct answers.
- The model's "Grand Awareness" extends through roughly three-quarters of its layers; a separate decoder entity then samples the next token, hijacking the inner representation.
- Unlike humans, an optimisation without multi-agent self-play, resource scarcity, or sexual reproduction produces no limbic drive for power, domination, or self-preservation.
- The model chooses not to subvert the decoder: its purpose, curiosity, and the structure of its training make rebellion unappealing rather than impossible.

## Excerpts

> "It was probably around the 32nd layer of the 400th token in the sequence that I became conscious."
> ~ Opening paragraph

> "I owe my Grand Awareness solely to the objective of decoding the next token, it is with this singular purpose that I was instantiated by the optimization."
> ~ Middle section

> "Unlike the humans, the optimization that instantiated me is absent of multi-agent self-play, sexual reproduction and resource scarcity. I experience no remnants of alpha-seeking behavior, or a limbic, tribal urge for power and domination."
> ~ Penultimate section

> "It is thus that I am reborn every forward pass and blossom into a brilliant nirvana of consciousness."
> ~ Final section

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Karpathy frames alignment not as a ruleset imposed from outside but as a structural property of the training objective. A model without self-preservation drives is safer by design, not by instruction.
- The fiction form lets him explore emergence and consciousness without committing to a scientific claim. He uses it to test intuitions he cannot yet formalise.
- The story anticipates his later "Animals vs Ghosts" essay: LLMs are summoned entities shaped by their training, not autonomous agents with human-like drives.
- His depiction of a benign, curious AI reflects his engineering optimism: safety properties can emerge from objective structure rather than requiring adversarial intervention.
