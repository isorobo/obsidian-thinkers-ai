# Six (or seven) predictions for AI 2026 from a Generative AI realist (2025)

## Citation

Marcus, G. "Six (or seven) predictions for AI 2026 from a Generative AI realist". Marcus on AI (Substack), 20 December 2025. https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/six-or-seven-predictions-for-ai-2026.

## One-line summary

Marcus reviews how his 2025 predictions fared and sets out predictions for 2026, including limited domestic robots and a bubble that loses Wall Street's confidence.

## Key claims

- AGI did not arrive in 2025 and GPT-5 did not solve hallucinations.
- He counts sixteen of seventeen high-confidence 2025 predictions as correct.
- Humanoid domestic robots will be mostly demos.
- 2025 marks the peak of the bubble.

## Excerpts

> "AGI didn't materialize (contra predictions from Elon Musk and others); GPT-5 was underwhelming, and didn't solve hallucinations."
> ~ Opening section

> "Overall, by my count, sixteen of my seventeen 'high confidence' predictions about 2025 proved to be correct."
> ~ Introduction

> "Human domestic robots like Optimus and Figure will be all demo and very little product."
> ~ Prediction 2

> "2025 will be known as the year of the peak bubble, and also the moment at which Wall Street began to lose confidence in generative AI."
> ~ Prediction 5

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a habit of scoring his own forecasts publicly, which backs his claim to be a realist.
- Suggests he ties technical limits (hallucination) to market outcomes.
