# How o3 and Grok 4 Accidentally Vindicated Neurosymbolic AI

## Citation

Marcus, Gary. "How o3 and Grok 4 Accidentally Vindicated Neurosymbolic AI". Marcus on AI (Substack), 13 July 2025. https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/how-o3-and-grok-4-accidentally-vindicated.

## One-line summary

Marcus argues that the latest frontier models work by importing symbolic algorithms into the workflow, quietly vindicating the neurosymbolic approach he has long defended.

## Key claims

- The cutting edge is tilting towards neurosymbolic AI, after a decade of leaders running the approach down.
- What has worked is importing ideas from neurosymbolic AI, including purely symbolic algorithms in the workflow.
- Enhancing LLMs with symbolic tools, once anathema, makes a large difference.
- OpenAI has accidentally vindicated neurosymbolic AI without public acknowledgement.
- Progress came from hybridisation, not from scaling neural networks alone.

## Excerpts

> "The cutting edge is finally, if quietly and without public acknowledgement, tilting towards neurosymbolic AI."
> ~ Opening argument

> "What has worked—to some degree—is importing some of the ideas from neurosymbolic AI, including using purely symbolic algorithms as a direct part of the workflow."
> ~ On what worked

> "In short, although LLMs are still far from perfect, enhancing them with symbolic tools, once an anathema, makes a huge difference."
> ~ On hybrid gains

> "OpenAI, without any sort of of public acknowledgement whatsoever, has accidentally vindicated neurosymbolic AI."
> ~ Conclusion

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The post reads frontier results as confirmation of a thesis he has held since at least 2018.
- Stressing the lack of public acknowledgement reflects his running grievance about credit in the field.
- Locating progress in symbolic tooling rather than scale restates the hybrid case in current terms.
