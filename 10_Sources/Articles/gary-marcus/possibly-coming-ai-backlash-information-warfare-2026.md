# The (possibly) coming AI backlash and information warfare (2026)

## Citation

Marcus, G. "The (possibly) coming AI backlash and information warfare". Marcus on AI (Substack), 6 January 2026. https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/the-possibly-coming-ai-backlash-and.

## One-line summary

Marcus predicts a political and public turn against pro-AI industry policy in 2026 and warns of eroding trust in what people see and read.

## Key claims

- He predicts the US president will distance himself from aggressively pro-AI policy by the end of 2026.
- He predicts generative AI will look like a fad.
- He warns that citizens may lose trust in much of what they read or see.

## Excerpts

> "By the end of 2026, President Trump will have begun to distance himself from the aggressively pro-AI industry policies"
> ~ Prediction section

> "Generative AI, once Silicon Valley's golden child, will start to look like a fad, a solution in search of a problem"
> ~ Prediction section

> "the fog of war may become more terrifying as citizens lose trust in much of what they read or see"
> ~ Information warfare section

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as forecasting that mixes politics and technology, with dated, checkable claims.
- Suggests he sees misinformation as the nearer harm compared with extinction.
