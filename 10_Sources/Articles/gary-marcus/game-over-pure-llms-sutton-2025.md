# Game over for pure LLMs. Even Turing Award Winner Rich Sutton has gotten off the bus. (2025)

## Citation

Marcus, G. "Game over for pure LLMs. Even Turing Award Winner Rich Sutton has gotten off the bus.". Marcus on AI (Substack), 26 September 2025. https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/game-over-for-pure-llms-even-turing.

## One-line summary

Marcus says Rich Sutton's recent remarks echo his own scepticism of pure language models, while they still differ on reinforcement learning versus neurosymbolic methods.

## Key claims

- He always thought Sutton's Bitter Lesson essay was overstated.
- He reads Sutton's recent podcast statements as a loss of support for the LLM camp.
- Both stress world models and the limits of pure prediction, but weight methods differently.
- He says only grifters still claim scaling LLMs is all you need.

## Excerpts

> "That said I have always thought it was wrong, or more to the point, overstated, and fatally flawed."
> ~ On Sutton's 2019 essay

> "When the LLM crowd has lost Sutton - and when Sutton sounds exactly like me - it's game over."
> ~ Reaction to Sutton's podcast statements

> "We both would heavily emphasize the need for world models, and the limitations of pure prediction, but he would put more weight on reinforcement learning than I would, whereas I would put more weight on neurosymbolic approaches and innate constraints."
> ~ Comparison of the two positions

> "Practically the only people still pretending scaling LLMs is 'all you need' are grifters."
> ~ Closing statement

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a pattern of claiming vindication when prominent opponents move toward his view.
- Suggests he is willing to name specific remaining differences with allies, such as reinforcement learning versus neurosymbolic methods.
