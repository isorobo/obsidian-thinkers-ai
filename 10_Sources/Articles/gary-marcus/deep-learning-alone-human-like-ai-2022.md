# Deep Learning Alone Isn't Getting Us To Human-Like AI

## Citation

Marcus, Gary. "Deep Learning Alone Isn't Getting Us To Human-Like AI". Noema Magazine, 11 August 2022. https://www.noemamag.com/deep-learning-alone-isnt-getting-us-to-human-like-ai/.

## One-line summary

Marcus argues that deep learning fails at compositionality and reasoning, and that understanding the origin of symbol manipulation should be the field's top priority.

## Key claims

- Deep learning on its own is the wrong tool for compositionality and reasoning.
- Any system that leverages both symbols and neural networks is, by reasonable standard, a hybrid.
- On natural language, compositionality, and reasoning, current systems remain massively unreliable.
- Symbol manipulation, once a dirty word among deep learning proponents, deserves renewed priority.
- Human-like AI requires combining neural and symbolic approaches rather than choosing one.

## Excerpts

> "Deep learning on its own is the wrong tool for certain jobs: compositionality, reasoning and so forth."
> ~ On deep learning limits

> "Any system that leverages both symbols and neural networks is by any reasonable standard a hybrid."
> ~ On neurosymbolic hybrids

> "On natural language, compositionality and reasoning ... these systems remain massively unreliable."
> ~ On reliability

> "In the 2010s, symbol manipulation was a dirty word among deep learning proponents; in the 2020s, understanding where it comes from should be our top priority."
> ~ On the historical shift

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The essay states his core distinction: pattern recognition is not the same as compositional reasoning.
- Defining "hybrid" broadly lets him claim that even rivals quietly rely on the symbolic ideas he champions.
- Treating reliability as the test of progress matches the robust-AI standard in his formal papers.
