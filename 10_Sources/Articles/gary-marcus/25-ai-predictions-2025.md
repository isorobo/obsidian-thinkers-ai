# 25 AI Predictions for 2025, from Marcus on AI

## Citation

Marcus, Gary. "25 AI Predictions for 2025, from Marcus on AI". Marcus on AI (Substack), 1 January 2025. https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/25-ai-predictions-for-2025-from-marcus.

## One-line summary

Marcus issues 25 dated, confidence-graded predictions for 2025, forecasting no AGI, no GPT-5-level leap, and thin model profits.

## Key claims

- There will be no artificial general intelligence in 2025, despite contrary claims.
- There may be no GPT-5-level quantum leap by community consensus throughout 2025.
- Profits from AI models will stay modest or nonexistent, while chip makers keep doing well.
- No single system will solve more than four of the Marcus-Brundage AI 2027 tasks by year end.
- Capability and economic limits will both persist through the year.

## Excerpts

> "We will not see artificial general intelligence this year, despite claims by Elon Musk to the contrary."
> ~ Prediction 1, high confidence

> "Profits from AI models will continue to be modest or nonexistent ... shovels will continue to sell well throughout the gold rush."
> ~ Prediction 3, high confidence

> "There could continue to be no 'GPT-5 level' model ... throughout 2025."
> ~ Prediction 25, low confidence

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Confidence-graded, falsifiable predictions show his preference for testable claims over rhetoric.
- The "shovels in a gold rush" framing restates his economic scepticism about model profits.
- Forecasting no AGI and no GPT-5 leap extends his standing thesis that scaling has plateaued.
