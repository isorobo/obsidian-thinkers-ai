# Five quick updates about that Apple reasoning paper that people can't stop talking about (2025)

## Citation

Marcus, G. "Five quick updates about that Apple reasoning paper that people can't stop talking about". Marcus on AI (Substack), 17 June 2025. https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/five-quick-updates-about-that-apple.

## One-line summary

Marcus defends the Apple reasoning paper against rebuttals and says nobody has answered his own essay on the replies.

## Key claims

- The paper showed LLMs break down on moderately complex versions of basic algorithms.
- A popular rejoinder, co-written with Claude, was an error-ridden joke.
- No convincing reply to his Seven Replies essay exists.
- Coding performance falls to zero when contamination is controlled.

## Excerpts

> "The core of the paper challenged the idea that LLMs can reason on any deep level, and showed that they broke down on moderately complex versions of basic algorithms"
> ~ Opening update

> "'The illusion of the illusion' turned out to be an error-ridden joke. Literally."
> ~ Update on the rejoinder

> "Nobody has written a convincing reply to my Seven Replies essay, despite the fact that about 90,000 people have read it."
> ~ Update on his own essay

> "Performance on hard coding problems, an alleged area of LLM strength, drops to zero when you control nicely for contamination."
> ~ Update on coding and contamination

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Reads as a combative style that treats unanswered arguments as evidence of strength.
- Suggests he uses outside empirical papers as support for a long-held position.
