# Why the Collapse of the Generative AI Bubble May Be Imminent

## Citation

Marcus, Gary. "Why the Collapse of the Generative AI Bubble May Be Imminent". WIRED, 3 August 2024 (republished on Marcus on AI). https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/why-the-collapse-of-the-generative.

## One-line summary

Marcus argues that generative AI underdelivers on its promises and that the gap between valuations and earnings points to an imminent market correction.

## Key claims

- Generative AI, as currently built, does not work well enough and may never close the gap.
- Companies valued at billions may fold or be stripped for parts.
- Valuations have run to hundreds of times current earnings, far beyond what results justify.
- Few of the prior year's favourites will meet the expectations priced into them.
- The economics of the sector, not only the technology, signal a coming correction.

## Excerpts

> "Generative AI, at least we know it know, doesn't actually work that well, and maybe never will."
> ~ On fundamental limitations

> "Companies that are currently valued at billions of dollars may fold, or stripped for parts."
> ~ On financial impact

> "Few of last year's darlings will ever meet recent expectations, where estimated values have often been a couple hundred times current earnings."
> ~ On market expectations

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The essay joins his capability critique to an economic one: weak technology cannot support inflated valuations.
- Naming concrete consequences, folded companies and stripped assets, shows his preference for falsifiable predictions.
- Tracking the investment-to-revenue gap matches the economic view stated in his profile.
