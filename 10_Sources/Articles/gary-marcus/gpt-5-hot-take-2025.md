# GPT-5 hot take

## Citation

Marcus, Gary. "GPT-5 hot take". Marcus on AI (Substack), 7 August 2025. https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/gpt-5-hot-take.

## One-line summary

Marcus judges GPT-5 a modest step that stays within the pack rather than the long-expected leap, and stresses it is plainly not AGI.

## Key claims

- GPT-5 is part of the pack, not a giant leap forward; Grok 4 beats it on ARC-AGI-2.
- OpenAI omitted the ARC-AGI-2 comparison from its livestream, making the event look like marketing.
- Fans will find things to praise, but GPT-5 is not the huge leap people expected.
- GPT-5 is obviously not AGI.
- The release supports his prediction that no decisive GPT-5-level jump would arrive.

## Excerpts

> "But still part of the pack, not a giant leap forward (e.g. Grok 4 beats it on ARC-AGI-2 results)."
> ~ Assessment

> "OpenAI conveniently forgot to include this comparison (ARC-AGI-2) in their livestream recital of benchmark progress, which left the livestream looking like marketing rather than science."
> ~ On the livestream

> "GPT-5 is obviously not AGI."
> ~ Conclusion

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The benchmark-by-benchmark reading shows his habit of checking marketing claims against specific results.
- Calling the livestream marketing rather than science restates his suspicion of AI-lab hype.
- Treating GPT-5 as incremental confirms his standing prediction that scaling has plateaued.
