# Let's be honest, Generative AI isn't going all that well (2026)

## Citation

Marcus, G. "Let's be honest, Generative AI isn't going all that well". Marcus on AI (Substack), 12 January 2026. https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/lets-be-honest-generative-ai-isnt.

## One-line summary

Marcus lists reasons he thinks generative AI is underperforming: memorisation, limited measurable value and fading returns from scale.

## Key claims

- A large fraction of what LLMs do is memorisation.
- They add little quantifiable value to the world.
- Scaling is not going well and probably will not cure these problems.
