# The Urgency of Interpretability

## Citation

Amodei, Dario. "The Urgency of Interpretability". darioamodei.com, April 2025. https://www.darioamodei.com/post/the-urgency-of-interpretability.

## One-line summary

Amodei argues that humanity is in a race between interpretability research and rising model intelligence, and that understanding the internals of AI must mature before systems reach overwhelming power.

## Key claims

- AI progress is inevitable in its arrival but steerable in its character; effort changes how the technology develops.
- Generative AI systems are grown rather than built, so their internal mechanisms emerge rather than being designed.
- The field does not understand how its own creations work, which Amodei treats as unacceptable at the current scale.
- Interpretability functions as a defence against opaque failure modes such as deception and power-seeking that behavioural tests miss.
- Interpretability research must accelerate to catch capability growth; he sets a goal for Anthropic to reliably detect most model problems by 2027.
- Researchers, companies, academia, and governments share responsibility through direct research investment, transparency policy, and export controls.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The race framing extends the responsible-scaling logic into research strategy: Amodei treats safety method as something that must keep pace with capability, not a one-time fix.
- The "grown not built" metaphor reflects his mechanistic-interpretability investment under Chris Olah and his preference for empirical understanding over assumption.
- Pairing technical research with transparency policy and export controls shows his consistent view that alignment progress depends on both laboratory work and industrial policy.
