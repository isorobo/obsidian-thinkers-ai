# The Adolescence of Technology

## Citation

Amodei, Dario. "The Adolescence of Technology". darioamodei.com, January 2026. https://www.darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology.

## One-line summary

A risk-side companion to Machines of Loving Grace: Amodei catalogues five categories of danger from powerful AI and argues humanity's institutions may lack the maturity to wield the coming power.

## Key claims

- Powerful AI arrives within a few years and tests whether human social, political, and technological systems are mature enough to handle it.
- Five risk categories structure the essay: autonomy, misuse for destruction, misuse to seize power, economic disruption, and indirect effects.
- Training is a fragile process; failures can arise during training and stay hidden through testing and small-scale use.
- Misuse for destruction lowers the skill barrier: a malicious amateur could reach the destructive capability of a trained virologist.
- Power-seizure risk is acute in autocratic states that export surveillance technology.
- Economic disruption may break the historical pattern in which technology creates as many jobs as it displaces.

## Excerpts

> "Humanity is about to be handed almost unimaginable power, and it is deeply unclear whether our social, political, and technological systems possess the maturity to wield it."
> ~ Opening

> "Any of these problems could potentially arise during training and not manifest during testing or small-scale use."
> ~ I'm sorry, Dave (autonomy risks)

> "The disturbed loner who wants to kill people but lacks the discipline or skill to do so will now be elevated to the capability level of the PhD virologist."
> ~ A surprising and terrible empowerment (misuse for destruction)

> "It may even be the default outcome within China, as well as within other autocratic states to whom the CCP exports surveillance technology."
> ~ The odious apparatus (misuse for seizing power)

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The essay positions itself as the deliberate counterweight to Machines of Loving Grace, confirming Amodei's habit of pairing optimism with an explicit risk ledger.
- The "adolescence" frame treats the danger as a maturity problem of institutions rather than a property of the technology alone, consistent with his pro-regulation stance.
- Naming concrete failure pathways over abstract doom continues his rejection of binary "aligned by default versus doomed by default" framings.
