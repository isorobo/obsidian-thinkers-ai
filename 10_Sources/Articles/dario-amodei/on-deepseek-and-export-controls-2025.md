# On DeepSeek and Export Controls

## Citation

Amodei, Dario. "On DeepSeek and Export Controls". darioamodei.com, January 2025. https://www.darioamodei.com/post/on-deepseek-and-export-controls.

## One-line summary

Amodei argues that DeepSeek's efficient models strengthen rather than weaken the case for US chip export controls, because controls keep democratic nations at the AI frontier.

## Key claims

- DeepSeek's releases make export-control policy more existentially important, not less.
- DeepSeek-V3 reflects genuine engineering-efficiency innovation rather than a break in the scaling trend.
- Three dynamics govern AI development: scaling laws, shifting the cost curve, and paradigm changes.
- Scaling the training of AI systems yields smoothly improving results across cognitive tasks.
- Well-enforced export controls are the decisive mechanism that can stop China acquiring chips at the scale needed for parity.
- DeepSeek's researchers are not framed as adversaries; the concern is the authoritarian state they operate under.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- The essay restates the three-factor model of progress (compute, algorithms, paradigm shifts) Amodei used in his 2023 Senate testimony, showing a stable analytical frame across years.
- His distinction between DeepSeek's researchers and the state they serve mirrors his "race to the top" stance: rivalry over governance, not over individual talent.
- Treating export controls as the central lever continues the industrial-policy thread that runs through Machines of Loving Grace and the interpretability essay.
