# Dario Amodei

## One-line thesis

Scale plus careful alignment research produces powerful AI that remains controllable; industrial policy and responsible-scaling commitments hold the line until interpretability catches up.

## Current role and affiliations

Co-founder and CEO of Anthropic. Formerly Vice President of Research at OpenAI, where he led the GPT-2 and GPT-3 efforts. Before OpenAI, research scientist at Google Brain and Baidu under Andrew Ng. PhD in computational neuroscience from Princeton.

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

Plausible arrival of "powerful AI" by 2026 to 2027 on his Machines of Loving Grace definition: country of geniuses in a data centre. Avoids the label AGI; prefers capability-threshold framing.

### On takeoff dynamics

Continuous progression followed by a compressed decade of transformative application. Rejects hard discontinuity. Argues for a period during which multiple labs sit at the frontier together.

### On alignment difficulty

Alignment is tractable but non-trivial. Invested heavily in mechanistic interpretability under Chris Olah. Authored the Responsible Scaling Policy framework as a voluntary commitment mechanism.

### On economic impact

Expects compressed decades of biomedical and scientific progress within five to ten years of powerful AI. Treats the productivity question as downstream of the capability question.

### On policy and governance

Supports compute export controls, mandatory pre-deployment testing for frontier models, and government capacity building via AI Safety Institutes. Has testified to the US Senate.

## Intellectual trajectory

From computational neuroscience to deep learning safety. Coauthored "Concrete Problems in AI Safety" (2016) as a founding agenda document. Led scaling work at OpenAI through GPT-3. Departed with colleagues in 2021 citing direction disagreements. Founded Anthropic on a safety-forward commercial model. Published "Machines of Loving Grace" in October 2024 as a positive-case manifesto.
