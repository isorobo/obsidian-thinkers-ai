# Erik Brynjolfsson

## One-line thesis

Productivity follows a J-curve: downward at first as complementary investments lag, then rapid upward as organisations restructure; the Turing Trap is the risk that we build AI that imitates rather than augments humans.

## Current role and affiliations

Jerry Yang and Akiko Yamazaki Professor at Stanford. Director of Stanford Digital Economy Lab.

## Stated positions

### On economic impact

Productivity J-curve applies to GenAI; early measured gains understate steady-state. "Generative AI at Work" (2023) finds 14% productivity gains for customer support agents.

### On policy

Avoid the Turing Trap. Incentivise augmentation over automation. Measure AI impact at task level rather than occupation.

## Intellectual trajectory

MIT Sloan productivity research from the 1990s. "The Second Machine Age" (2014) and "Machine, Platform, Crowd" (2017) with Andrew McAfee. Stanford Digital Economy Lab 2020.
