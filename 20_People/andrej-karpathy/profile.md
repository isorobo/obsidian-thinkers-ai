# Andrej Karpathy

## One-line thesis

LLMs are a new kind of operating system; the software stack rebuilds itself around them; education is the bottleneck on adoption.

## Current role and affiliations

Pre-training researcher at Anthropic (joined May 2026), leading a team that uses Claude to accelerate pre-training research. Founder of Eureka Labs (AI-native education, 2024 to 2026). Former founding member of OpenAI. Former Director of AI at Tesla (Autopilot).

## Stated positions

### On capability framing

"LLM OS" conceptual model: the model as kernel, tools as system calls. Treats chat as one interface among many.

### On alignment

Engineering problem; observability and evals scale with capability.

### On economic and educational impact

Reconstructs how software is built and how it is learnt. Founding thesis of Eureka Labs.

## Intellectual trajectory

Stanford PhD under Fei-Fei Li. CS231n instructor. OpenAI founding member 2015. Tesla Autopilot 2017 to 2022. Return to OpenAI 2023. Eureka Labs founded 2024. Joined Anthropic pre-training team May 2026.
