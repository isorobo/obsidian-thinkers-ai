# Gary Marcus

## One-line thesis

Large language models plateau on the hard parts of cognition; the field has bet on the wrong architecture; neurosymbolic hybrids are the path; meanwhile the commercial bubble will correct.

## Current role and affiliations

Emeritus professor of psychology and neural science at NYU. Founder of Geometric Intelligence (acquired by Uber) and Robust.AI.

## Stated positions

### On capability

LLMs exhibit persistent reasoning failures; hallucination is not an incidental bug; scaling alone does not fix it.

### On the architecture question

Neurosymbolic integration required. Pure connectionist bets do not close the gap.

### On policy

Strong pre-deployment regulation; FDA-style approval for high-stakes applications.

### On the commercial cycle

Investment-to-revenue gap signals a correction; critical of OpenAI and Anthropic valuations.

## Intellectual trajectory

Cognitive scientist. "The Birth of the Mind" (2004). Founder of Geometric Intelligence (2014, acquired by Uber). Public LLM critic from GPT-3 onward. "Taming Silicon Valley" (2024).
